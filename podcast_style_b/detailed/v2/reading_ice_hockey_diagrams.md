### 0. What this is

A conversion, not a rewrite. Every idea and every fact of the old script is kept, in the old order, and the old script's own Notes §6 remains the fact-by-fact source trace. The changes are format (two voices, a scene for each mark, the player's questions carrying the reasons, terms defined at first use, "what would you do?" moments, three recaps given back by the player), and a small number of wording changes that follow source changes since the old script was written (§5), plus two safety additions (§5).

### 5. Wording changes that follow the source, and safety additions

The old script was written against RID blob `b38fd4d`; RID is now `a9c71d0` (`a6aa87c`). The diff `133b0a7..HEAD` was read in full. It adds, at RID :100–:101 and KT10 :293–:295, "Where body checking is banned, a hit is a penalty, and a hard one can get you thrown out of the game", and in the trailer "plan on a match penalty and a likely multi-game suspension" for USA Hockey.

- **Ejection limb.** Old: "And where checking is banned, a hit can get you thrown out of the game." Now: "Where checking is banned, a hit is a penalty, and it can get you thrown out of the game, especially a hard one." This keeps the old script's "a hit can" (so nothing is made cheaper) and adds the source's "a hit is a penalty" and "a hard one". Voiced twice: third idea and tenth idea (the curled bracket), where the source now puts it (RID :100–:101).
- **Not voiced** (owner's less-rules rule, no book ladder): the match tier and the likely multi-game suspension (trailer :299). The old script did not voice the match tier either (old Notes §4).
- **Who is in a no-check game.** Old Notes §4 named this as the omission a listener could be caught by, and said "if one thing goes back in, make it those two thresholds in one sentence". Added in the third idea, without book names: "In many places checking is banned for players around twelve and under, in girls' and women's hockey, in non-check adult leagues, and in other divisions a league chooses." Source: RID :9–:13 (USA Hockey 12 and under, girls' and women's, non-check adult; Hockey Canada U13 and below, female, "and any other divisions approved by a Member"). The last limb was added on safety review (§9). "Around twelve and under" covers USA Hockey's "12 & under" and Hockey Canada's "U13 and below". "And the answer can even change from one game to the next, depending on who you play" is unscoped, as in the old script and FS :16 ("can change game to game"); RID :11–:12 gives the USA Hockey case. The first draft scoped it "in some leagues"; the safety review removed that (§9).
- **"Never hit from behind / can't see you coming" added to the third idea body.** The old script had it in the spine and marks recaps but not in the third idea's own gate. Added, since the route-on-opponent act is taught there.
- **"…and it can end your game"** added to the injury reason in the second idea. FS :22 ("Every book here can end your game for it"), :38.
- **Never-your-back and boards line added to F one (second idea):** "Never turn your back to anyone coming at you, anywhere on the ice. And if the puck is on the boards when you get there, keep your head up, your chin off your chest, and never duck." (Wording from the safety review, §9, so the never-your-back rule is not scoped to the boards.) Old Notes §4 flagged that "F one goes to the puck" could be heard as a boards retrieval with no posture limb. Source: FS :1024 ("Never turn your back to anyone coming at you, and never duck… head up and chin off your chest"), :1049 ("It holds in every league, forechecking and pinching alike"). The full boards posture (skates parallel, forearm and hip) is not voiced; this episode teaches no boards act.
- **"Don't push or shove a player either. That can be a penalty in any league."** Kept as the old script's reviewed wording (old Notes §0, MJ1). Old Notes §6 recorded that "in any league" is wider than RID's trailer, which supports it for no-check divisions only. Not re-checked here.
- **The "light hit… head or neck" reason** stays as the old script had it. FS :1049 now reads "a head-down collision with the boards can injure your neck even at walking speed". No injury response is taught, so the head-and-neck red-flag list in the writer brief does not apply.

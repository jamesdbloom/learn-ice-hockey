# SG73: site look — quote-bracket breaks, a redundant flag, and two amber runs (24 October 2026)

## What changed

- **`"[` line breaks (site/src/plugins/remark-corpus.mjs).** A straight quote followed by a bracket (the disclosed-insertion convention, `"[a]ny…`) is a line-break opportunity in Chromium. A site-reviewer measured 97 of 858 instances splitting at 400px across 35 pages. The plugin's final text pass, beside the 🇬🇧 NBSP pass, now inserts a WORD JOINER (U+2060) between them. The change is rendering-only: md_to_speech and the markdown are untouched. In the built HTML, 858 of 858 are joined and 0 break at 400px.
- **conditioning_and_recovery.md :29 (Key focus).** Removed "🇬🇧 " before "**In the UK…**". It was the only glyph in the Key focus layer and repeated what the next words say. md_to_speech voices 🇬🇧 as "For British readers." (md_to_speech.py:345), so the listener no longer hears that phrase before "In the UK…". It was redundant, because the next words scope the sentence. The commit gate rendered it and confirmed this. No ⚠ was involved (check_marker_pairs: 0 lost).
- **special_teams.md Common Mistakes :1200 and :1209.** The ⚠️ amber run had covered a whole multi-sentence penalty tariff: 13 and 11 lines at 400px, with the instruction in plain weight. The ⚠️ and the bold now sit on the instruction ("Make your room with position and a screen, never by initiating contact with the goalie" / "Go for the puck, not through the back"), and the tariff follows in plain weight. No word changed. Each paragraph keeps its one ⚠️, so its spoken "Important." stays.

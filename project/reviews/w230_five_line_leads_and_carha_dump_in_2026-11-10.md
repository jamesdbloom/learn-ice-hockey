## Source
- **Owner decision, 9 November 2026:** coaching sections lead with the tactic. After W228 the census found 105 panel-first coaching sections, 62 of them with 5-line panels. This wave took the 53 with 5-line panels outside the three files reserved for the CARHA row.
- **Plan row from W229:** under CARHA 66(a) Note 1 the last player to touch the puck, other than a goalkeeper, is in possession. Note 2 gives "stand their ground" / "shadow" only to the defending players. So a forechecker chasing their own team's dump-in gets no lane.

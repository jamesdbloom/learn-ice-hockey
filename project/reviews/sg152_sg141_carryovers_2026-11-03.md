## What changed
- **"Head first" now names its object.** It reads "head first into the boards or goal frame" (USA Hockey 608(b)) wherever it was bare:
  - body_contact Key focus :31, body :776, facts :1203 and Key Takeaway 6;
  - center facts :754.
  
  Without the object, any head-first fall read as an ejection.
- **body_contact facts :750, "never hit from behind".** It is now a per-book list:
  - NHL, PWHL, Hockey Canada and CARHA eject;
  - IIHF, plan on it;
  - USA Hockey starts at a minor plus a misconduct, and ejects if forceful at the boards, head first into the boards or frame, or reckless.
  
  The old opener, "no bare minor", could be heard as scoped to three books. CARHA, which the old line also left out, is now included.
- **body_contact facts :751 (Hockey Canada).** The old line tied the 7.5(a) floor to a head-first condition. It now gives:
  - 7.5: a minor plus a game misconduct, a major if the player is hurt, and a match if driven defenceless into the boards or frame or if meant to injure;
  - the head-contact tiers, with "any high cross-check" stated unconditionally (7.6(b) and 7.7(b), "whether or not injury results").
- **center Common Mistakes :821 (goalie hit from behind).** It now covers:
  - CARHA: a game misconduct at least (53(a));
  - USA Hockey: at least a minor and a misconduct, an ejection if reckless or head first into the net or boards (608(b)), and a mandatory match for excessive force on a defenceless goalie (608(c)).
  
  Before, the line was silent on USA Hockey.
- **defensive_zone_coverage :209 and defender :300.** Each page now says once that in every book with a match penalty it keeps you out of later games until your case is dealt with, and under USA Hockey out of practices too (NHL and PWHL 21.2, USA Hockey 405(c), Hockey Canada 4.10(a), CARHA 34(a)). defensive_zone_coverage keeps CARHA 59(d) (owner ruling, 31 Oct). The IIHF writes no match penalty.
- **defender :351–:353.** The bullets now lead with the instruction:
  - "Lift their stick with yours; never grab it." USA Hockey, Hockey Canada and CARHA penalise any hold.
  - "Take the lift low, never at the hands."
  - "Lift the stick; never chop at it."
  
  The tariff text is unchanged.
- **rules_primer.**
  - The CARHA 30(a) accidental high-stick exception now says "a player's", not "a skater's", because the rule covers goalkeepers (:376, :1064, :1142).
  - The 66(b) citation was added for "any deliberate contact with one can" (:810).

Closed from the SG141 row without a separate edit: bcb :753 not listing "reckless" is covered, because :750 and :754 both carry it now; rules_primer :810 "costs you the game" was already gone from the text (stale row item).

## Wrong premises the triage found (corrected in the plan)
1. **Hockey Canada washed-out minor.** The SG172 row cited HC 5.6(e) for "a minor wiped out by a goal still counts toward three". 5.6(e) only tells the scorer to advise the referee of a second misconduct or a third stick/head-contact penalty. Interpretation 6 to 4.15(b) records the surviving half of a washed double minor "as a Minor penalty" and does not settle counting. No Hockey Canada rule settles it; the CARHA half (39(b) Note 1) is verified. The plan row now says so.
2. **"Five of six".** The SG160 row asked whether defending_the_rush :434/:1016 and defender :903 read USA Hockey as the exception. They do not: the excepted book in defending_the_rush is the IIHF ("plan on it"), and defender no longer carries a "five of six" hit-from-behind line. Closed.
3. **Boarding.** No site states boarding as the only double minor counting as two (body_contact :95, time_and_space :356 and rules_primer :616 name it in context). SG172 row item (2) is closed in the plan.
4. **special_teams :1139.** "HC 8.5(b) wherever the goalie stands" is not a defect: "A Major penalty and Game Misconduct penalty will be assessed to any player who charges the goaltender" has no location limit. Removed from the not-swept list.
5. **dzc :500.** The row listed it as a candidate. It errs harsher (HC ejects where a 7.6(a) minor is possible), not cheaper. The plan row now says so.
6. **team_play line numbers** had drifted (:397/:637/:639 → :399/:639/:641), and KT5 carried the same claim. All four were fixed in this wave, and the SG142 carry-over row now marks them done, along with the team_play swearing split, passing :440's second-head-contact limb and dzc :182. The SG139–SG140 row marks goaltender :1376's head-first scope done.
7. **Not in the plan:** defending_the_rush KT9 and CM :1018 said USA Hockey makes "any reckless" hit from behind a match. 608(b) prices reckless at a major and game misconduct, and 608(c) needs excessive force on a vulnerable player. Corrected in this wave.

## The ruling
IIHF 43.1 does not settle how a cross-check into the back of a player who sees it coming is priced. Its definition is a check on a player "not aware of the impending hit". Its next sentence then brings a hit from behind "in any manner (i.e., high-sticking, cross-checking, etc.)" under the rule.

On 28 October 2026 the owner chose **"Plan on Rule 43"**. Under the IIHF, which is Britain's book, a cross-check into the back is to be planned as checking from behind, which has no minor, whether or not the player sees it coming. The NHL and PWHL keep the awareness split: a seen cross-check there is a 59.2 / 60.2 minor. The ruling is also recorded in memory.

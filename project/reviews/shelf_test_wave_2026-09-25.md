## The method: the shelf test

🔴 **Does a book count match the shelf the document ITSELF declares?**

⚠️⚠️ **AND IT SAYS "CHECK THE COUNT", NOT "RAISE THE COUNT". That half is what keeps it safe.** Three times this
wave the obvious widening would have been FALSE:
- **CARHA writes no onus sentence at all** (`grep -ci onus` = 1, and that hit is equipment maintenance). The line
  reads **five of six, plus CARHA 49(a)**, not six.
- **The faceoff caption's two missing books sit on OPPOSITE SIDES of the split** — PWHL 78.7 permits skate contact
  with the line, CARHA's Glossary counts a skate *on* the circle as encroachment. A bare count change would have
  been wrong in both directions at once.
- **A Key Takeaway naming an envelope could not take the PWHL**, because PWHL 52.1 states a *different* envelope.
  ***"This needs a writer, not a count."***

**Counts tested and correctly LEFT ALONE, repeatedly:** four ACTS not four books · comparatives among named books ·
a section on checking permission by classification · trailer provenance describing what was read on a named date ·
~30 comparisons in one document that DECLARES its narrower set in terms. ***"A mixed count really is not evidence
on its own."***

### 🔴 The blind spot, named by the agent that used the test best
> ***"A caption or sentence that states a four-book shelf by ENUMERATING FOUR BOOKS AND NEVER WRITING A NUMBER
> scores ZERO against a string census. Given the shelf grew from four to six, ENUMERATION-WITHOUT-A-NUMBER is the
> LIKELIEST REMAINING SHAPE, and finding it needs a per-caption read — NOT A GREP."***

**Hunted deliberately afterwards, it produced findings in every file it was aimed at** — a permission scoped with
the word *"only"* to three books that never named the fourth; a facts line naming two books where three write no
minor; four boarding sites enumerating three where four write the balance.

## 🔴 A repair manufactured a critical, and this shape had no name before

**An earlier correct edit widened *"under BOTH the NHL's rulebook and the IIHF's"* to name three books — in the
FIRST HALF of a sentence-run — and left *"both books waive"* standing in the SECOND HALF.**

⚠️⚠️ **THE WORD *"both"* WAS THE ANTECEDENT THE TRAILING CLAUSE HUNG ON.** The paragraph then named three books and
said "both". **The act is a deliberate turn of the back into an oncoming check — this corpus's own *"the position
that breaks necks"* — and PWHL 43.1 waives it. A PWHL reader was told she still drew the power play.**

🔴 **This is NOT the propagation failure already on record.** That one is *a correction that reaches a layer and
stops*. **This one reaches a clause and breaks the clause beside it.** The standing test — *"which layer was
written assuming the old answer?"* — cannot see it, because the layer is the same sentence.

✅ **THE TEST THAT DOES: after widening a count, re-read the WHOLE sentence-run for quantifiers that were bound to
the old one.** An agent later predicted the identical shape in its own repair, unprompted — *"if it is ever demoted
to another layer, 'it' travels without the clause."*

## 🔴 What a second read inherits

> ***"I took the brief's 'already cleared' list ON TRUST, which is the one thing the brief told me to do and THE ONE
> THING THIS PROJECT'S OWN HISTORY SAYS IS MOST LIKELY TO BE WRONG. If that earlier read mis-cleared a claim, I
> REPRODUCE THE ERROR SILENTLY."***

**A second review scoped to new text inherits the first review's errors by construction.** Worth weighing before
scoping the next one that way — the saving is real, and so is the exposure.

## 🔴 The shape this wave manufactured THREE TIMES, from three correct edits

**A correct widening leaves a quantifier bound to the old count, elsewhere in the same sentence-run.**
- The first removed *"both"* from one half of a run and left *"both books"* in the other — **permissive, on a
  deliberate turn of the back into a check.**
- The second widened a paragraph's opening to *"four of the six books read here write it"* and left *"the other
  three books write"* standing **~2,400 characters later in the same paragraph** — conservative, and caught by a gate.

⚠️⚠️ **THIS IS NOT THE DOCUMENTED PROPAGATION FAILURE.** That one is *a correction that reaches a layer and stops*.
**This reaches a clause and breaks the clause beside it**, so the standing test — *"which layer was written assuming
the old answer?"* — cannot see it: the layer is the same sentence.

✅ **THE TEST: after changing a count, re-read the WHOLE sentence-run for quantifiers bound to the old one.**
✅ **THE DURABLE FIX: NAME THE BOOKS INSTEAD OF COUNTING THEM.** The repair agent chose names deliberately —
*"my sentence cannot be stranded by a future widening of the shelf, which is the defect I was sent to fix, twice
manufactured in this file."*
⚠️ **And one site was found carrying the claim with NO quantifier at all.** ***"Leave it quantifier-free"*** — a
future editor "making it consistent" by inserting a count would manufacture the defect a third time.

- The third widened a count and **SILENTLY MOVED THE ANTECEDENT**: *"The books split three ways… one of **the
  three**"* became *"The six books read here split several ways… one of **them**"*, where *"them"* now sits beside
  *"the six books"*. 🔴 **The count looked widened; the referent changed from WAYS to BOOKS, and the only reading a
  listener has became *"one of the six books penalises you"* — which is false.**
  ⚠️⚠️ **AND THE FALSIFYING EVIDENCE WAS IN THE SAME COMMIT: a facts line this wave ADDED says the IIHF prices the
  ducker too.** **The diff shipped a facts line and a Common Mistakes bullet that contradicted each other, inside one
  file.** **Permissive, for the British reader, on a cervical-spine bullet.**

⚠️⚠️ **THREE FOR THREE. EVERY COUNT EDIT IN THIS FILE STRANDED SOMETHING, and each was caught by a DIFFERENT
reader — a safety review, one gate, and the next gate.** **None was caught by the agent that made the edit, and none
by any checker.**

⚠️⚠️ **THE METHOD'S LIMIT, NAMED BY THE AGENT THAT APPLIED IT:** ***"A stranded count written as 'both books', 'each
of them', 'neither book' or 'the rest' leaves NO LEXICAL TRACE OF A NUMBER and my method cannot see it — which is
exactly how the clause I repaired survived."***

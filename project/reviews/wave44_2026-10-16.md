## What changed

Eight lines changed.

- **winger facts :471:** "a CARHA major ejects" implied that a Hockey Canada major does not. It now reads "a CARHA or Hockey Canada major ejects (30(a), 8.5(e))".
- **pwtp :901 CM, :974 KT11, :646 facts:** Hockey Canada's mandatory ejection for a charge on the goalie had been limited to the crease. It now applies anywhere (8.5(b) has no location condition). Interference with a goaltender that injures is a mandatory ejection under Hockey Canada (8.5(b)) and CARHA (66(e), 30(a)).
- **winger :489:** a fouled attacker who makes no effort to avoid the goalie takes at least a minor. In either book, it is a major and an ejection if the goalie is hurt.
- **goaltender facts :1152, body_contact :1260, glossary :363:** the Situation 5 permission now carries both of its conditions: only while the goalie has the puck, only to win it, and never to intimidate or punish.

## Why
Every page header says "Wherever this page gives the IIHF rule, it is yours — amended locally" (SG95). A read-only rules-verifier audit screened about 650 IIHF-bearing lines against the In-House Rules 2026-27 and the EIHL Casebook. It found no un-noted amendment that would get a British reader penalised or hurt, and 11 low-stakes gaps. These are the worthwhile ones.

## What changed
- **rules_primer :376.** "Five minutes, then back on" now also covers a plain willing fight in the NIHL, SNL and BUIHA Varsity (In-House Section 6), with the exceptions in the same sentence:
  - an instigator or aggressor is still ejected (an automatic game misconduct);
  - so is an NIHL fighter in the last five minutes or overtime;
  - anyone who keeps going after being told to stop, or joins as third man in, can be (both discretionary).
- **rules_primer :1048 (table cell).**
  - Juniors: "play junior hockey as though it does too (an inference from Rule 201, not its words)".
  - Neck guard: "(a warning, then a minor, in the Elite League)", from EIHL 9.12.
- **on_ice_communication :322.** No coach's challenge or video review "in EIH or SIHA competition" (In-House 37/38).
- **how_to_watch_hockey :275.** Outside the Elite League, the centre is replaced for every faceoff violation except after an icing (In-House 76).
- **bcb**
  - :1324 and :1334: EIHL Casebook 42 compels a game misconduct on a major for a face or head injury.
  - :373 and :390: v1.1 60.1 and Situation 60.6 added, so the high-stick claim cites both editions; the reference is the opponent in both.
  - Header: "more of those differences".
- **shooting :859.** "the EIHL can suspend or fine (Casebook 5.6)".
- **team_play :485 and Sources.** The EIHL's automatic minimum three-game suspension for leaving the bench to join an altercation.
- **goaltender :937.** In-House 9.8's helmet-off misconduct does not mention goalies, so expect the IIHF goalie stoppage and ask your officials.
- **getting_started :193.** "the IIHF rules in these documents, as amended locally, are yours".

## Carried to the plan
- shooting :859 says "the EIHL can suspend or fine", but Casebook 5.6 says "shall be subjected to". That understates a mandatory sanction.
- rules_primer :517 says third man in and persisting "keep their IIHF ejection". Both are discretionary, so the line overstates them in the harsh direction.
- faceoffs :799 calls it unsettled whether lateness costs the centre, and says "plan on losing the dot". IIHF 76.7 says the centre is not ejected for lateness. This errs harsh.
- shooting and bcb on EIHL 86.6: the Casebook contradicts itself on whether warm-up offences are called on the night.

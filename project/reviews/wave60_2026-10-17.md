# Wave 60 (17 October 2026): the 21.1 sweep's census hits, Hockey Canada head contact, helmet grabs, and "damage limitation" labels across eight documents

## What ran

Claim briefs (scratchpad b60/_CLAIMS.md):
- **A:** NHL/PWHL 21.1 and CARHA 48(a) make a deliberate attempt to injure a mandatory match; USAH 602(a) means "plan on a match" for reckless endangerment.
- **B:** Hockey Canada 7.6/7.7 head-contact tiers.
- **C:** helmet and facemask grabs, priced book by book.
- **D:** the facing-the-wall fallback labelled as damage limitation.

Each document was owned by one agent. Agents refuted two brief errors:
- HC 7.6(a)'s double minor is for *intentional* contact, not "moderate violence".
- NHL 75.2(v) can apply Rule 21 for a grab, so the NHL minor is not "the whole of it".

The brief was corrected.

### Recorded against the method

Each repair round shortened a ladder and dropped a mandatory limb, and a fresh reader caught it each time: "can" for "shall", a lost antecedent, a narrowed floor. Across three rounds this happened about eleven times. All of them leaned permissive, and none survived to this commit as far as the readers found. This confirms CLAUDE.md's warning that summarising a tariff overstates it toward the simpler rule, and that the fix for one site does not propagate on its own.

Two agents read Hockey Canada from the superseded print in waves 41–42. The coordinator re-checked every HC clause they relied on against `hc.txt`, and all were identical.

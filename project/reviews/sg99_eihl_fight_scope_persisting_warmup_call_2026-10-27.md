## What changed and why
- **rules_primer :517.** The willing-fight ejection is now scoped to the IIHF book. The line adds that the Elite League's Casebook differs: a first fight is at least a major, with the game misconduct on a second major ("count the season") or on any fight joined after the first has started (EIHL 46.1, 46.2).
  - Third man in can eject you at the referee's discretion, even without throwing a punch (IIHF 46.14 / v1.1 46.7).
  - Under the IIHF book, a third man who fights takes the fighting major and its ejection too (46.1, 20.4).
  - Persisting after being told to stop can also eject you.
  - The old line told an Elite League player that a plain willing fight ejects, which the Casebook does not do.
- **uk_rules :138.** The 2025/26 persisting tariff now carries its own "at the referee's discretion" (v1.1 46.1).
- **shooting :888.** Adds that an Elite League referee who sees a warm-up offence may call it (Casebook 86.6, closing paragraph).

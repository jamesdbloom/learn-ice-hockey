## Why
Earlier repair waves on 28 October (SG112–SG116) had grown the checking-from-behind and head-contact penalty ladders back onto these three non-owner pages, as SG117 had found on Defensive Zone Coverage and Winger. This follows two owner rules: rules are background and tactics come first, and each extra restatement is one more place for a correction to miss. So each page keeps one full statement where a player meets the act. Elsewhere it gives the instruction, a short true tariff and a link to Body Contact and Battles. Each author agent ran a per-limb layer test and named the surviving carrier of every limb it removed.

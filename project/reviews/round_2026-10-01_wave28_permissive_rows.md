## What was wrong, in kind

- **neutral_zone_systems.** Charging named a mandatory ejection only for USA Hockey, Hockey Canada and CARHA.
  - Now added: NHL/PWHL 42.5 and EIHL Casebook Rule 42 (game misconduct mandatory once a charging major injures the face or head).
  - The first repair said only that "the IIHF lets the referee add one to a reckless charge". That understated the head case: IIHF 42.1 is superseded by the head-check rule, where 48.3's only major carries an automatic game misconduct. Now in all five layers.
  - Hockey Canada 7.4(e): every HC charging major ejects.
  - Also fixed: CARHA 79's injury tier on the hard dump, and PWHL 52.1 step-or-glide.
- **faceoffs.**
  - "A clean tie-up" and "neutralise it" taught no method. Every layer now says: lift or press their stick with your own, then play the puck; never a hand on their stick, your stick on their hands, a hook or a hold-down.
    - Holding the stick: NHL/IIHF 54.2, PWHL 55.2, USAH 622, HC 8.1(a), CARHA 63(a).
    - Stick on the hands: IIHF 55.1, HC 8.2(a) Interpretation 1.
    - Hooking a stick: USAH 623 and HC 8.2 only. NHL, IIHF and PWHL 55.1 exempt stick-to-stick contact, and CARHA writes no such rule.
  - CARHA 49(b)'s double minor and its 32(a) count were added on the boards sites.
- **goaltender.**
  - A chop or cross-check in the crease was "the same two minutes".
  - It now runs: two minutes at least; a major plus game misconduct where severe or reckless; a major that must be called where it injures (HC 9.2(b)/9.3(b), CARHA 54(c)/80(b), and for a slash NHL 61.3 / PWHL 63.3).

  - A match penalty in every book but the IIHF: mandatory under HC 9.2(c)/9.3(c) and CARHA 54(d)/80(d), and reachable for reckless endangerment alone under USAH 609(c)/634(c).
  - A charge on a goalie out of the crease is still a charge (IIHF Situation 42.2).

  - The PWHL 61.4 high-stick match penalty is added beside NHL 60.4.
  - "A minor, or a double minor" is now "at least a minor".

  - The slew-footing heading now reads "ordinary" battle position, not "legal".

## The lesson

Every block in this wave was the contrast shape again: a list or a qualifier that left a reader of an unnamed book, or an unnamed situation, hearing a cheaper price.
- **Named books:** the IIHF beside NHL/PWHL/EIHL; USA Hockey beside HC/CARHA.
- **Unnamed situations:** early-game fighting beside late-game fighting; the In-House instigator beside the EIHL instigator.

Three of those blocks were introduced by coordinator nits and sketches. **Nit-level rewording requests are new text and carry the same risk as repairs; brief them as constraints, not strings.**

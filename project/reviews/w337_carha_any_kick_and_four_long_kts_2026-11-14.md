## What changed
- **risk_management :756 (facts, 277/300) and :905 (body) (E).** CARHA now reads "plan on a match for any kick at an opponent". The basis is 48(c): "A player who attempts to kick or deliberately kicks an opposing player shall be assessed a Match penalty", which has no direction limit. The text also discloses that "the book's own ruling makes a kicking slew-foot from behind a match at the referee's discretion (86(b))", from the 86(b) Case Situation: "with a kicking motion from behind … RULING: At the discretion of the Referee". A foot simply left out stays an 86(a) trip.
  - The old carve-out was this guide's specific-over-general reading, presented as the book's text. The rules-verifier confirmed it was also inaccurate: 86(b) does reach a kicking slew-foot, so "that 86(b) does not reach" could not define 48(c)'s scope.
  - "Plan on" is the conservative, honest instruction. The wording avoids both forms W333 rejected: "any other kick" and a "must" that swallows the from-behind case.
- **defensive_zone_coverage KT4 :927 (F), 131 → 122 words.** The five-book ladder is replaced with:
  - the corpus cost clause;
  - "Under USA Hockey it does whenever it is forceful at the boards, sends them head first into the boards or net, or is reckless";
  - "Plan on being ejected if it reaches their head or neck, too";
  - the Hockey Canada 7.5(c) and CARHA 53(b) match into the boards;
  - pointers to The base shape and What it costs.

  USA Hockey's minor-plus-misconduct floor is never voiced alone.
- **winger KT11 :971 (G), 113 → 119 words.** The faceoff KT now:
  - adds "head" to the instruction;
  - turns the per-book list into "a minor in three books and can be in the rest" (USA Hockey 613(e), Hockey Canada 6.2(c) and CARHA 57(b) mandatory; NHL/IIHF 76.7 and PWHL 78.7 discretionary);
  - gives Hockey Canada 6.2(d)'s eligibility bar its own sentence;
  - adds USA Hockey 620(b) to the helmet clash ("an ejection if it is careless or worse"), where the old KT gave only the 620(a) floor;
  - points to At the dot.

  The ⚠️ moved from the eligibility clause, a procedural cost, onto the helmet-clash hazard. The KT is longer, not shorter, because review restored limbs (below).
- **winger KT10 :970 (G), 115 → 104 words.** "under Hockey Canada and CARHA hold only the ice you already have, and avoid contact". CARHA's "shadow" permission (66 Note 2) is dropped from the KT, which is stricter; it remains at :698, :705, :839 and :848.
- **rules_primer KT1 :1192 (H), 117 → 101 words.**
  - USA Hockey's tag-up scope (630(d)) now sits next to "everyone tags up", so the KT no longer says tag-up applies to everyone before qualifying it.
  - The four-book boarding/charging clause (NHL/IIHF/PWHL 41.1, Hockey Canada 7.2 preamble) is compressed to "most books".
  - USA Hockey's boarding and charging prices now include the reckless tier (603(b), 607(b)). The old KT voiced only the floor.
  - **rules_primer Common Mistakes :1128** gains the same reckless tier.

  1. "Take it yourself and…" sounded like an order to the player just told they cannot take the draw. It became "If you take a draw yourself, …".

## What changed

### A. rules_primer: the claim in every layer
The claim is Hockey Canada's head-contact ejection tiers. It was briefed as a claim, not as a line.

**Overview :31 and Common Mistakes :1154 (identical):**
> "Under Hockey Canada it must if it injures the player in minor and female hockey or is highly violent in Junior and Senior, where any foul or illegal hit that injures a player's head on the glass, ice or boards must too. So must a cross-check above the shoulders at every level, injury or not. At every Hockey Canada level a head hit is a match if the player cannot protect themselves or you meant to injure them."

**KT5 :1206** uses the safety reviewer's wording, which puts the division first:
> "…Under Hockey Canada it must if it injures in minor and female hockey; in Junior and Senior, if it is highly violent or any foul or illegal hit sends a head into the glass, ice or boards and injures it. Under Hockey Canada a cross-check above the shoulders must at every level, injury or not. At every Hockey Canada level a head hit is a match if the player cannot protect themselves or you meant to injure. ⚠️ **Hit in the head? Come off and say so:** …"
- The concussion limb is byte-identical.
- "without any intent to injure" became "without intent", which matches the Overview.

**Body :206.** The boards limb now has its own subject: "…and so is any foul or illegal hit that sends the player's head into the glass, ice or boards and injures it."

**Table cell :1087** (checking from behind) gains "or you meant to injure them" on its head-match limb.

**What each repair fixed:**
- **Subject mismatch fixed.** At W377 the subject was "it" (a head hit) while the boards trigger is any foul. The boards trigger now has its own subject.
- **Antecedent fixed.** "them" became "the player", so each unit reads correctly voiced alone.
- **Attempt-to-injure match added** in every summary layer: 7.6(c) "attempts to or deliberately injures" and 7.7(c) "deliberately attempts to injure". It had been missing before W377 too.
- **High cross-check added** to the "must" list (7.6(b)¶3 / 7.7(b)¶2, "above the normal height of their shoulders … whether or not injury results"). This was the safety reviewer's Major. The gap predates W377: heard alone, the list implied a high cross-check with nobody hurt was not a mandatory ejection.
- **Body :479 left as it is.** Its subject is the illegal hit itself, so the narrow form is exact there.

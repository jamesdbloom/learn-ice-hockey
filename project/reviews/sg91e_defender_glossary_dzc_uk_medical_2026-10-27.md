## What changed and why

### Instruction-first ladder bullets: defender and dzc
Single-book ladder bullets were reordered so that each leads with what a player does, then gives the tariff. The rules-verifier and safety-reviewer found that four of the new leads had taken a tariff trigger as the instruction (a qualifier changing jobs as it moved). All four were repaired:
- defender :326 "never send them in head first" became "pull up; any hit from behind is at least a minor plus a misconduct (608(a)), and head first or forceful at the boards ejects". It now also carries the Casebook 608 Sit. 1 quote.
- defender :305 "without leaning on them" became "without making body contact". CARHA 49(a) prices failing to avert any body contact.
- defender :493 "Pull up at the wall" became "Pull up — at the wall a forceful check ejects…".
- defender :495 "Keep it off the head" became "Pull up, and never reach their head". Under HC 7.5(a) a hit from behind ejects even off the head.
- dzc :177, :195 and :204, "hold the pin for help": every site now carries USA Hockey's limit. More than three seconds held against the boards is a minor whether help is coming or not (632(b); Casebook 632 Sit. 6).
- dzc :203 "Pin the puck, never the opponent" was a tactic stated as a rule (HC 7.5 Interp. 3 permits a low-speed pin). It now reads "In this system, pin the puck, not the opponent".
- defender :329 and :496: "no checking-from-behind minor" (NHL, PWHL and IIHF 43.2).

### Glossary (Cross-document ladder demotion, leftovers)
- :287 and :417: the ladders were shortened.
- :287 says USA Hockey "names no stick: a stick shove with the force of a check can be checking from behind (608), and a lighter one to the back is still cross-checking (Casebook 609)".
- 7.5(c) keeps its "cannot protect themselves" condition.

### The UK concussion route (owner: research all three questions)
- **Northern Ireland has no NHS 111** (NIAS FOI 36395-25; nidirect). uk_rules :451, KT23 and conditioning :334 now give GP or GP out-of-hours there, sourced to nidirect's concussion page.
- **Scotland's sportscotland 2024 guidance agrees:** 111 is NHS 24, no competition before day 21, and 999 for red flags. It prints thirteen red flags where the UK guidance prints fourteen, because it joins the two consciousness flags.
- **Voicing:** "111" is now always preceded by a dialling verb, so the renderer says "one one one" and never "one hundred and eleven".

### British U10/U12 checking: conditioning :608
- The unscoped "Where it is barred, a check is at least a minor" is now scoped to USA Hockey and Hockey Canada.
- The British junior rules name no penalty for a plain check but bar it. At U10 the official can remove you for the shift and the coach should remove a repeat offender. A check that boards, charges or hits the head is a penalty at any age.

### The U20 mouthguard: equipment KT5
IIHF 202.3 applies only without full face, and every IHUK junior (U10–U19) must wear full face. So 202.3 never reaches a British junior.

## Cross-corpus repair: the PWHL trapezoid

revK found that PWHL Rule 65.2(viii) still makes it a minor for a goalkeeper to play the puck
outside the designated area behind the net, while Rules 1.8 and 27.8 switch the trapezoid off.
This wave had added the PWHL to "books with no trapezoid" in about eighteen documents. Three
agents repaired the claim in every layer of every file that carried it; the owner document,
goaltender.md, had read the contradiction permissively ("treat the trapezoid as gone") and was
changed by coordinator ruling to the conservative reading ("do not count on the freedom"),
because where a book is silent or contradicts itself the conservative reading is the one that ships.
A dedicated reviewer then read all 68 resulting sites: one critical (defender KT7, where the PWHL clause was spliced in front of the no-trapezoid instruction "the rim is your goalie's"), two majors (goaltender "restrict the freeze instead"; glossary attacking side told "play as if it binds") and seven minors — all repaired before this commit.

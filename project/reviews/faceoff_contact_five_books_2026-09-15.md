## ⚠️⚠️ THE CLAIM WAS NOT UNDER-PROPAGATED. IT WAS FALSE.

**A gate blocked because a CARHA whole-blade finding had reached the body of `faceoffs.md` and
stopped.** The repairing agent was sent to propagate it into four further layers. **It refuted the
finding instead.**

> **CARHA Rule 57(a) ¶1** — *"with the full blade of their sticks flat on the ice"* (`carha.txt:2701`)
> **CARHA Rule 57(a) ¶2** — *"The sticks of both players facing-off shall have the toe of the blade

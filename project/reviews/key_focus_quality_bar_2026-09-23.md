# The Key focus quality bar — 23 September 2026

## The owner's instruction, and what changed

> *"I'm happy to relax the key focus rules, we can even have **up to 10 points** but as long as each
> point is **truly a key focus and important for tactical or strategy of how to play effectively**. I
> want the key focus to concentrate on **the things a player should most focus on to play effectively
> and be a good player**."*

⚠️ **The *"four or five"* cap is gone. The test is whether each point EARNS ITS PLACE.**
⚠️⚠️ **AND IT RAISES THE BAR: a count cap could be met by cutting; a quality bar cannot. A weak
bullet is not made right by being under ten, and it was not made right by being under five.**

## 🔴🔴 THE FINDING, MEASURED ON 35 DOCUMENTS: THE DEFECT IS ABSENCE, NOT EXCESS

**Four audits ran. Together they made 51 ADDITIONS against 9 CUTS and 6 DEMOTIONS.** Bullet counts
rose in every group. ⚠️ **The first audit warned the rest: *"A report that only cuts has probably
only asked half the question."*** **It was right four times out of four.**

**The examples are the argument:**
- ⚠️⚠️ **`body_contact_and_battles.md`'s Key focus said NOTHING A PLAYER DOES TO WIN A PUCK** — on
  the corpus's battle document.
- ⚠️⚠️ **`risk_management.md` OWNS the owner's own worked example — *"never pass the puck, or skate
  it, across the front of your own goal"* — AND ITS KEY FOCUS DID NOT STATE IT.**
- ⚠️⚠️ **`defensive_zone_coverage.md` has a section titled *"What Stays True Across Every Coverage
  System"* listing SEVEN universals. ITS KEY FOCUS CARRIED THREE** — and the missing four included
  **stay on the defensive side of the puck**, the owner's other register example.
- **`time_and_space.md`'s was entirely OFFENSIVE**, though half the document is about taking time and
  space away. **`playing_without_the_puck.md` barely described playing without the puck.**
  **`center.md` never mentioned protecting the slot. `defender.md` sent you to the goalmouth and
  never said what to do there. `goaltender.md` opened on depth and never said BE SQUARE.**

## ✅ THE DISCRIMINATOR FOR TECHNIQUE, now in CLAUDE.md

> ***"Does a teammate watching a shift notice this, or only a coach watching your feet?"***

**Key focus:** edges · change of speed and the first three steps · knee bend · eyes up · stopping on
both sides · puck on the far side · carrying without decelerating · cushioning a pass · release speed
· shot location · arriving at a battle with position.
**Mechanics, and they belong in the body:** push direction and the toe flick · stride recovery · grip
pressure · blade cupping · the deke catalogue · hollow depth.
⚠️ **The owner's non-example, *"dragging your skates"*, is on the mechanics side; *"you never change
speed"* is on the Key focus side. THE EXCLUSION IS NOT A BAN ON TECHNIQUE.**

## ✅ AND THE RULING ON LOOK-UP INSTRUCTIONS

> *"**Yes, and it is often the strongest point on the page — but only when the look-up IS the action,
> not a substitute for one.** The test: would a reader who does nothing but this bullet be better
> off? *'Ask your league which book you are under'* passes — there is no on-ice way to derive it,
> getting it wrong is an ejection, and the answer changes what every other bullet means. *'Find out
> what your team plays'* as a trailing hedge on a tactic FAILS."*

⚠️ **Corollary on record: `uk_rules.md` measures 100% rules-bearing in Key focus and `rules_primer.md`
96% AFTER the work, and both are correct. DO NOT DRIVE EITHER DOWN.**

> *"I'm happy with both of these, I agree **in both cases in this context the language is good**, but

document the hazard is ABOUT and too much detail in a document that merely inherited it.

belongs in Key focus."** **Both agents reasoned from the text of the exclusion; the discriminator

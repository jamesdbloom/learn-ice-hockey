## What changed

### Claims that told a reader an act was cheaper than the books make it

- **Holding under CARHA.** CARHA 63(a) lets the referee make any hold a major, which ejects under 30(a). puck_handling's facts line and winger's Common Mistakes had left this out.
- **Slew-footing.** It ejects in five books once called, not four (CARHA 86(b)). Changed in risk_management KT9.
- **The kick shot.** NHL, IIHF and PWHL 49.2 and Hockey Canada 6.10(c) all wave off the goal. on_ice_communication's facts line had said only two books reach it.
- **Pulling the goalie early.** It is a stoppage only while the goalie keeps going. NHL, IIHF and PWHL keep a minor "in all other situations", and USA Hockey Casebook 205 Situation 1 makes it too many men (team_play).
- **Hockey Canada Junior post-icing draw.** A second violation is a mandatory minor (6.5(b)), so there are two mandatory cases, not one (special_teams).
- **Failure to take position at the dot.** NHL 76.3(i), IIHF 76.3(I) and PWHL 78.3(i) all say "shall have the offending center replaced". center had said only Hockey Canada was automatic.
- **Goalie putting the puck out of play.** CARHA 55(a) has a goalie limb, parallel to Hockey Canada 10.1(v) (DZC).
- **Holding under USA Hockey.** The match penalty for reckless endangerment is mandatory (602(a)). It had been shortened to "possible".
- **Hit from behind into the boards on a player who cannot protect himself.** Mandatory match under Hockey Canada 7.5(c), CARHA 53(b) and USA Hockey 608(c). zone_entries Common Mistakes had said "can be".
- **A goalie freezing the puck in his crease.** The NHL, IIHF and PWHL test is "unless actually being checked", not "unpressured" (special_teams facts).
- **The empty-penalty-box icing carve-out.** PWHL 83.6 carries the NHL 81.6 limb word for word. It was named as NHL-only at about 15 sites in seven documents.
- **Goalie icing wave-off.** PWHL 83.3 carries it too (goaltender).
- **Hockey Canada 6.11(d)(i).** It can whistle a knowing offside play on the defending carrier. zone_entries and breakouts had said nothing stops play.
- **A punch.** Under USA Hockey one punch is a fight. Elsewhere, a punch to the head is head contact (Hockey Canada 7.6 via 7.9; CARHA 50(b)), which can be a major and an ejection. Under CARHA a fight is a match for the instigator or aggressor, and brings a suspension (59(a), 59(d)). The text had said "every book ends your game", and an intermediate repair said only "still a penalty".

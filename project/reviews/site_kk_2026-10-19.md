# Site row (kk) and dependabot row (y), 19 October 2026

## Changes (site/ only)

- **Phone audio bar.** Under 40rem the bar is slimmer (68 px) and stays in the page flow until the first `playing` event, then sticky (`html.audio-idle`, set in essentials-view.js). Scroll padding follows it.
- **Contents tracking.** An IntersectionObserver marks the current section with `aria-current="location"` in both contents lists, skipping headings hidden by the Essentials view. Only the desktop rail is styled, because the inline list sits above every heading.
- **Figures lifted out of `<p>`.** 659 empty `<p>` went to 0. Spacing now comes from the existing `h* + figure` rules.
- **npm audit fix, no --force.** 6 packages, all build- or dev-only: devalue, fast-uri, http-cache-semantics, sharp/libvips, smol-toml, source-map-js. No major bumps; the audit now shows 0. The lock file keeps HEAD's libc fields.
- **Analytics (report only).** Production injects GA4 via PUBLIC_GA_ID (deploy.yml → consts.ts → BaseHead). It was confirmed on the live home page. Stale comments are fixed in the W commit. infra/cloudfront.tf:120-121 is left for owner approval.

## Review

A site-reviewer used headless Chrome, with same-origin iframes at 320 and 400 px and the browser at 1280 px, on centre, goaltender, puck_support and core_principles, in light and dark themes.

**CRITICAL (fixed).** The idle bar under `position: relative` kept `top: var(--header-h)` and overlapped the section label and the H1 on every page on phones. Fixed with `top: auto`. Re-tested at 320 and 400: no overlap, and no jump on play at the top of the page.

**MINORs (fixed):**
- The inline contents highlight could never be seen, so its styling was dropped.
- A failed play cleared `audio-idle`, so it now clears on `playing`.

**Passed:**
- Contents tracking: 164 samples, 0 disagreements, no jitter, and it is correct in the Essentials view.
- 330 figures, 0 empty `<p>`; spacing matches the documented values.
- No horizontal scroll.
- Print gives the full page.
- The console is clean.

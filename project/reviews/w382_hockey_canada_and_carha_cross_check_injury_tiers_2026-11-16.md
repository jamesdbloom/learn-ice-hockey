## Source
The W381 read-only census of cross-checking (record `w381_carry_overs_head_contact_suspensions_and_carha_head_butt_2026-11-16.md`).
- **No Key Takeaway was wrong.** 13 sites across 9 pages priced a Hockey Canada or CARHA cross-check below the book. The usual shape was USA Hockey 609(b)'s "reckless" mandatory tier stated beside HC/CARHA, with their injury tier (HC 9.2(b)¶2, CARHA 54(c)) left out.
- **Four pages never stated either book's injury tier:** on_ice_communication, defender, language_and_glossary and puck_handling.
- **Corrections to the coordinator's ground truth:**
  - HC 7.6(b) Interpretation 2 limits the minor/female above-the-shoulders tier to "a deliberate cross-checking action". Sites that carry "deliberate" are correct.
  - CARHA 54(c) is a major **and game misconduct**, and has two limbs: a goalie in the crease, or any cross-check that injures.

W382 took the six census files outside W381. The census items on W381's own pages (winger :796, defender :324/:326/:931/:313, body_contact :2076, shooting :532) are carried forward.

# Wave 61 (17 October 2026): USA Hockey's reckless-endangerment match paired with 602(a) across thirteen documents

## The claim

USA Hockey writes sixteen act-specific match limbs as "A match penalty for reckless endangerment may also be assessed for X": 603(c), 604(e), 606(b), 607(e), 609(c), 611(c), 619(b), 620(c), 621(c), 622(c), 623(c), 628(c), 634(c), 635(b), 639(c) and 640(h). Its general rule 602(a) says one "shall be assessed to any player or team official who recklessly endangers or attempts to injure any opposing player". Waves 58–60 settled the convention, and this wave applies it: state the limb, and pair it in the same voiced unit with "plan on a match (602(a))", scoped to a reckless act. Where the goalie is the victim, the wording is "expect a match".

604(e) attaches to the plain body check, not only the reckless one. Every site keeps that scope and puts only the "plan on" behind "if reckless".

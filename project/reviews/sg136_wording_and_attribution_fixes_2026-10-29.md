## What changed
**body_contact_and_battles**
- :834 said CARHA **32(d)** "gives a game misconduct". The game misconduct comes from **30(a)**. 32(d) attaches the automatic one-game suspension. The line now reads: "Every major there carries a game misconduct, unless it is for an accidental high stick that injures nobody (Rule 30(a), Note to 62(b)), and ⚠️ outside an accidental high stick, that game misconduct automatically brings a one-game suspension…" The injury clause was added after review (see C11).
- :624 "Three of the books make a match mandatory for a hit from behind" sat next to "every book but the IIHF" and named its three books only after the wider count. It now opens "Three books — CARHA, Hockey Canada and USA Hockey — make a match mandatory for the worst hits from behind". The NHL/PWHL 43.4/21.1 limb and the every-book-but-IIHF intent limb now follow USA Hockey. No limb was lost.

**goaltender**
- Facts :1378 "a major and game misconduct if hurt" had lost its subject. It now reads "if they are hurt", with the book name moved to the front to fit (297/300).
- Facts :1374 "eject hurt or not" now reads "eject, injury or not". The 608(c) clause was reworded to "(USA Hockey 608(c): likely a multi-game ban)" (298/300).
- An intent-to-injure route on :1374 was not added. Without a book name it would imply an IIHF match penalty, and the line is at its cap. Carried.

**puck_handling**
- "four above a minor" became "four books start above a minor", at facts :492, body :531 and Common Mistakes. The NHL and IIHF can also go above a minor, through Rule 21, so "start" removes the contradiction.

**forechecking_systems**

- :767 "In every book here, the floor for a hit that stays on that back is an ejection" was untrue for USA Hockey's minimal pinch (Casebook 608 Situation 1). Final text, matching KT9: "Called as checking from behind, that hit ejects you in every book here but USA Hockey, and there too when it is forceful along the boards." See C11 for the two intermediate wordings.
- Key Takeaways and Common Mistakes were checked by hand; all lead with the instruction. :658 measures 1,197 characters, so its row is closed.

**defensive_zone_coverage**

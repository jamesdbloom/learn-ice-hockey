**team_play_and_culture.**
- **Measurements.** check_tactics_ratio is 49% (Key focus 0%, Overview 0%, Common Mistakes 50%, Key Takeaways 56%). Prose is 14,715 words. The page renders to 44 chunks. The five "What it costs" units total 2,267 words, about 9 of 44 chunks.

- **Better targets the author identified:**

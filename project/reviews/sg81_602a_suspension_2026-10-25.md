## What changed and why
The owner ruled on 19 October that wherever "plan on a match" is stated for USA Hockey 602(a), the likely multi-game suspension is added. USA Hockey's Standardized Discipline Policy puts 602(a) at Level 1, with a "recommended range of a 6-10 game suspension" (usah.txt ~:1204-1212). Matches at Levels 2 and 3, cited alongside it, are also multi-game, at 4–8 and 3–5 games.

A census found 139 sites with no suspension within about 150 characters of a "602(a)" "plan on a match". Five agents, each owning a disjoint set of files, added the clause:
- body text: "and a likely multi-game suspension";
- facts lines: "…and a likely multi-game ban".

Sites already carrying the suspension were skipped, as were sites where the match belongs to Hockey Canada, CARHA or the NHL. To keep facts lines under the 300-character cap, agents reworded them; no limb was removed.

Cap-blocked facts lines were left unchanged and are listed as a plan row: bcb :577/:578/:675, forechecking :625/:626, OZP :646/:926/:931, winger :518/:519/:520/:748, game_management :900. Each needs a restructure, or should accept the page-level statement under the 404(b) once-per-page model.

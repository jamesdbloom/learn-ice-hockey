## What was wrong, in kind

- **CARHA tiers left unsaid.** These were missing from the documents where a CARHA player meets the act:
  - the charging tiers: 52(a) "more than two steps or strides", and a mandatory major plus game misconduct on injury or on a goalkeeper in the crease (52(b));
  - 49(a)'s intentional-contact prohibition and its "does not avert body contact" limb;
  - 49(b)'s double minor and its count toward 32(a);
  - 63(a)/(b) holding majors;
  - 79's fifteen-inch slap-shot limb and its fake-shot penalty;
  - 66(b) and 66(e) for contact with a goalkeeper.

  In each case a major ejects under 30(a). Several sentences said "the two books that give a stride count", or "four books", where CARHA is a third or fifth.
- **Goalie contact out of the crease.** zone_entries said CARHA's charging rule did not reach a player out of the crease ("its 66, not its 52"). That is false: 52 reaches it through the 52(b) Note, the 52(a) stride Note and the location-free injury limb. The body copy survived the first repair of the facts and Common Mistakes copies, and was caught by the read.
- **A book named as "the strict one".** offensive_zone_play called CARHA the book that "goes further" and bars standing in the crease. Hockey Canada 8.5 prints the identical clause, and USA Hockey 625(b) is flat. The repair then said CARHA, "as Hockey Canada does, but" with "no incidental-contact allowance". That implied Hockey Canada has one; HC 8.5(a) Interpretation 1 disallows any goal after attacker-initiated contact. A third repair scoped the allowance to the NHL.
- **Empty net and hooking.** risk_management offered "a foul that costs a minor, not one from behind". With the net empty, the NHL, IIHF and PWHL award the goal for any foul that denies the chance. The bullet also named the injury major without its ejection in four books (NHL/PWHL 55.4/56.4, HC 8.2(b)/(e), CARHA 30(a)).
- **Holding as a ceiling.** rules_primer said "the ceiling is a minor in the NHL, the IIHF, the PWHL and USA Hockey". NHL/PWHL 21.1 and USAH 602(a) reach a match penalty. Three successive repairs were each blocked and re-read before it was right:
  - the sibling bullet named only the NHL;
  - the NHL/PWHL 21.1 quotation dropped "attempts", beside USA Hockey's "attempts to injure";
  - the strength-move permission was said to be missing only from USA Hockey, when CARHA lacks it too and the PWHL writes it.
- **Force against an official.** rules_primer gave only the NHL/IIHF game misconduct. The new sentence then priced USAH/HC/CARHA as a match penalty "not a game misconduct". That left an NHL or PWHL reader hearing the lesser price. NHL and PWHL Rule 40 add automatic suspensions (Category II: at least ten and four games), and IIHF 40 adds supplementary discipline. The sentence now says so.
- **USA Hockey 608 and 620; Hockey Canada 7.6/7.7.**
  - 608(b)'s two limbs and 608(c)'s mandatory match were restored where one limb stood alone.
  - USAH 620(b) "intentionally or carelessly" and HC 7.6(b)/7.7(b) "whether or not injury results" were routed to the head-height cross-check.
  - HC's minimal-accidental carve-out is now confined to minor and female hockey and floored at the 7.6(a) minor.
  - NHL/PWHL/IIHF 43.2 "no minor" and 43.5/43.3 "game misconduct on any major" were added where checking from behind was priced.
- **The stick tie-up.** center taught "tie up the stick, not the puck", which, voiced alone beside hand advice, invites holding the stick (NHL 54.2; HC 8.1(a); CARHA 63(a)). Every layer in center and offensive_zone_play now reads "lift or press their stick with your own, then play the puck — never hook it, hold it down or grab it with your hand".
- **The box-out.** playing_without_the_puck and zone_entries assumed "a legal box-out". Under CARHA 49(a) it can be a penalty. Both now read "legal or not". The same assumption stands in four other documents and is carried as one row.

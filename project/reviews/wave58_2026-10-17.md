# Wave 58 (17 October 2026): the NHL/PWHL 21.1 sweep across six documents

## The claim

NHL 21.1 and PWHL 21.1: "A match penalty shall be imposed on any player who deliberately attempts to injure or who deliberately injures an opponent in any manner." CARHA 48(a) is the same ("shall be assessed"). USA Hockey 602(a) says "shall" for "recklessly endangers or attempts to injure"; the corpus voices its reckless limb as "plan on". The act-specific clauses (NHL/PWHL 42.4, 43.4, 51.3, 59.4/60.4; USAH 607(e)) say "may" and are not the whole answer. The IIHF, the EIHL Casebook and the In-House Rules write no match penalty.

Wave 57 found Neutral Zone Systems pricing a charge to injure by 42.4's "may" alone. The claim was briefed to every carrier. This wave covers six documents; risk_management, playing_without_the_puck, defending_the_rush and shooting are in wave 59.

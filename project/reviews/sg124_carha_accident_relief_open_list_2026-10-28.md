# SG124: CARHA's accident relief is an open list; head contact scoped to no stick (28 October 2026)

**Files:**
- content/technique/body_contact_and_battles.md
- project/plans/OPEN_ITEMS.md

## Why
Body Contact and Battles stated CARHA 49(c) ("Where, in the opinion of the Referee, accidental contact has taken place, no penalty shall be assessed") as a closed list: accidents are free except at the head and from behind into the boards. That was false in the permissive direction, in three ways:
- **66(b)** penalises contact that impedes a goalkeeper "by actual physical contact", with no intent element.
- **86(a)** penalises a player who "falls or slides along the ice directly in the path of the puck, causing the puck carrier to trip", at a minor or a major plus game misconduct.
- **50(a)'s minor for accidental head contact** applies only "other than a stick". The Rule 50 Note sends stick contact to 62(b), "a Major penalty, whether or not injury results".

The SG122 rules-verifier found the 66(b) gap at CM :1996. The SG124 reviews found the rest.

## What changed
Seven units, final text:
- **facts :94:** "49(c) excuses accidents, but head contact (50), contact impeding a goalie (66(b)) and sliding into a carrier (86(a)) are penalties; driving a defenceless player from behind into boards or net, a match (53(b))". 300/300.
- **:121:** "intent matters more than touch — but not every accident is free: contact to the head, contact that impedes a goalie, a slide that trips the puck carrier before you play the puck (86(a)) and a hit from behind into the boards are still penalties". Before, it said "the line is intent rather than touch".
- **:312 and :461:** "but not every accident is free: without a stick, contact to the head, face or neck is still a minor (50(a)), and driving a defenceless player from behind into the boards or net is a match (53(b))".
- **:794:** "49(c) excuses accidental contact, but not all of it: head contact without a stick is a minor (50(a)), as is contact impeding a goalie (66(b)); a slide that trips the carrier is at least one (86(a)); a defenceless player driven from behind into boards or net, a match (53(b))".
- **CM :1996:** "even accidental contact is not always free — contact to the head, contact that impedes a goalie or a slide that trips the puck carrier before you play the puck is still at least a minor, and from behind… on a player who cannot protect themselves, a match". Before, it said "only accidental contact goes unpenalised".
- **facts :475:** "mandatory if intended contact injures". Before, it said "mandatory on injury", but 49(a)'s mandatory major applies only "When injury results from intentional body contact". The 49(a) "stands in front" quotation became a faithful paraphrase.

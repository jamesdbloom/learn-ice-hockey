# Wave 75 (18 October 2026): Hockey Canada head contact "up to a match" now carries the mandatory defenceless match

**The defect.** Many units priced Hockey Canada head contact (7.6; 7.7 Junior/Senior) as "up to a match" without saying the match is mandatory. 7.6(c) and 7.7(c) read: "A Match penalty will be assessed to any player who hits a player in the head in such a way that the player is unable to protect or defend themselves or … attempts to or deliberately injures". "Up to" made that mandatory match sound like a ceiling.

**How it was fixed.** Five fixers (A–E) each took a disjoint file set and read every "up to a match" site on its own. Each site was marked FIXED, ALREADY CARRIES or NOT THIS DEFECT. The brief told fixers not to rewrite a correct unit into the long form, because readable beats defendable.

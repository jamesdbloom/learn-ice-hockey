## Why
The owner ruled on 25 October that every USA Hockey match penalty on the Standardized Discipline Policy carries "a likely multi-game suspension", with 602(a) enough as the citation. SG81–SG83 reached most sites. These facts lines were left at their 200- or 300-character caps. An earlier squeeze was reverted because it dropped Hockey Canada's head-first ejection, the IIHF "major" and USA Hockey's "reckless" scope. This wave restructured the lines instead of squeezing them. Three agents worked on disjoint files.

## What changed
- **body_contact_and_battles**
  - **Checking block.** Two coaching-line merges freed slots. :573 is play the body with elbows and hands down. :574 is now "Never: Hit a player turned toward the boards or whose back is to you, anywhere on the ice, or reach upward or outward to hit" (HC 7.5(a) "anywhere on the ice"; USAH 608 Note "in open ice or directly into the boards").
  - **Checking from behind and kneeing.** Each was split into an other-books line and a USA Hockey line (:576–:579).
  - **Head contact.** :675 and :676 were re-divided. "Reckless" was added to the 620(b) mandatory major plus game misconduct. The Casebook 620 Sit. 4 quote is kept. The 620(c) "may also be assessed" quote was dropped; :675's 602(a) "shall" covers it.
  - **Other splits and relabels.** :1427 is a new standalone 608(c) mandatory match. :1702/:1703 split the holding line. :1704 Never → Rule ("Rule: Never touch an opponent's facemask…"). The :1679 kneeing table cell is unchanged, because adding the clause would make the renderer drop the table; :579, :620 and :1662 carry it.
- **defender**
  - :465 is the other books.
  - :466 is USA Hockey 608(b), Casebook 608 Sit. 1, 602(a), and the 608(c) mandatory match on a defenceless player.
- **winger**
  - :518 and :519 are regrouped by topic. 607(b) was dropped; it was a mandatory major plus game misconduct when reckless, and the harsher 602(a) match replaces it.
  - :520 cites HC 7.6(b), not 9.2(b) Interp. 2, which only points to 7.6. 609(b) is replaced by the 602(a) match.
  - :748 already complied.
- **breakouts :177**
  - "A USA Hockey screen for the carrier is a 625(a.1) minor; hitting an unaware or defenceless player is boarding, charging, checking from behind, head contact, roughing; if dangerous, careless or reckless with no effort to play the puck, a major and game misconduct or a match and likely multi-game ban."
  - "Unaware" is the Preface's own defining word (:322–331). The page's body (:223) gives a forechecker watching the puck as its example.
- **passing :483/:484 and puck_handling :291/:292** are split by book (kicking).
  - USAH 627(b) "kicks, attempts to kick or recklessly endangers an opponent by kicking".
  - HC 7.1(c)(iii); CARHA 48(c).
  - NHL and PWHL 49.3 match, injury or not; IIHF 49.3 major plus automatic game misconduct.
- **puck_handling :294** is tightened with every citation kept.
- **puck_handling :490/:491** split the holding line. 622(c) is scoped to the cage, and Casebook 622 Sit. 3 adds a mandatory match for fingers inside that twist or pull. "Any reckless hold" rests on 602(a).
- **Left unchanged**
  - OZP :646. No rewrite keeps all four Casebook tiers within 300 characters; :645 and the body at :666 carry the clause.
  - OZP :926 and :931 were already done.

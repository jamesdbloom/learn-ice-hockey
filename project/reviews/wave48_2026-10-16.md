**MAJOR.** pwtp facts :640 had been compressed into "penalises unnecessary contact". Voiced alone, that makes Hockey Canada sound like the NHL, IIHF and PWHL line before it, and it loses HC's distinctive duty: attackers "must make an effort to avoid contact in all circumstances… The onus is always on the attacking player" (hc.txt 8.5). It is fixed at 300/300: "Hockey Canada 8.5 requires an effort to avoid a goalie always, anywhere, and ejects if a foul on one injures (8.5(b))". The fix was re-derived from hc.txt ~:6985–6990 and has had no fresh reader since. The other pwtp summary layers (CM :901, KT11 :974) were checked.

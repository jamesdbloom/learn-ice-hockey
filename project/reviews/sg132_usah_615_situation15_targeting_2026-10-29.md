## Why
USA Hockey 615(c) gives a game misconduct plus a two-game suspension to a player "whose actions during an altercation causes the removal of an opponent's helmet and facemask". Casebook 615 Situation 15 adds: "During an altercation, Player A deliberately targets the helmet, or head area, of the opposing player who is properly wearing their helmet. As a direct result … the opposing player's helmet comes off … A match penalty is assessed to Player A." Four pages priced causing an opponent's helmet to come off at the game misconduct only. That was permissive for a deliberate act; found in SG131's review.

### Commit-gate block and repair
The first gate BLOCKED on bcb :1939. The coordinator's paraphrase "whose actions in an altercation **knock** an opponent's helmet off" narrowed 615(c)'s "causes the removal" in the lenient direction. In Casebook 615 Situation 17, Player B takes the game misconduct and two games where a loose strap let the helmet come off with no deliberate act. The record had also misread Situation 17 as "harmless". The coordinator rewrote the line to "…a game misconduct and a two-game ban, on top of any other from the incident, to a player whose actions in an altercation cause an opponent's helmet to come off — a match penalty and likely multi-game ban if they deliberately targeted the helmet or head (Casebook Situation 15)" (300/300). On the gate's note, it also moved a misplaced gloss in the team_play :744 Sources trailer back beside Situation 15's exemption.

## Carried to the plan

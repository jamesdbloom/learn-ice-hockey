## IIHF facts the wave rests on (verified by the rules-verifier in both editions)
- **No match penalty.** "match penalt" scores 0 in iihf_rules_v1.1.txt, iihf_rules_2026-27.txt and both Situation Handbooks.
- **No player attempt-to-injure rule.** 40.2/40.3 concern Officials only. 21.1 Dangerous Actions reaches only acts "not covered by the playing rules".
- **Tiers by act:**

  | Rule | Act | Wording |
  |---|---|---|
  | 41.4 | boarding | "may" major + GM |
  | 42.4 | charging | "may" major + GM |
  | 43.2 / 43.3 | checking from behind | no minor; "at their discretion, shall assess a major penalty and an automatic game misconduct … who recklessly endangers" |
  | 44.3 | clipping | "may" major + automatic GM |
  | 49.3 | kicking | "may" major + automatic GM |
  | 50.3 | kneeing | "may" major + automatic GM |
  | 57.4 | tripping | "may" major + GM |
  | 59.3 | cross-checking | "may" major + automatic GM |
  | 69.2 | goalkeeper interference | minor or major, no GM |

- **Supplementary discipline:** 41.5, 43.4, 44.4, 49.4, 50.4, 57.7, and 23.2: every game misconduct is reported to authorities with "full power to impose further disciplinary measures".
- **NHL and PWHL 21.1:** "A match penalty shall be imposed on any player who deliberately attempts to injure or who deliberately injures an opponent in any manner". 43.4 is "may".
- **British layers:** the EIHL Casebook and the In-House Rules add no match.

## The settled wording
- **Where the IIHF clause is a "shall" (43.3):** "…the IIHF, which has no match penalty — plan on ejection there (43.3)".
- **Where it is a "may":** name the tier, for example "plan on a major and game misconduct (42.4)" or "a major with an automatic game misconduct at the referee's discretion (49.3)", plus "supplementary discipline can follow" where the unit could be heard as the whole cost.

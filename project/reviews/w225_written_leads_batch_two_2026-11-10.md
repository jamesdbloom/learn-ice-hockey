## Source

## What changed
Each section below now opens with a one- or two-sentence lead placed directly under its heading, above its facts panel:
- **special_teams:**
  - "You may ice the puck freely": every book's limits, in one sentence.
  - "The two penalties a kill talks itself into": clear hard and flat, off the glass not over it, never fall on the puck.
  - "The goalie's role on both": never freeze a retrieved puck; the trapezoid applies outside USA Hockey, Hockey Canada and CARHA, British hockey included.
- **goaltender**, "Shootouts": stay in your crease until the shooter touches the puck. The existing body paragraph was folded into the lead.
- **defender:**
  - "Net-front technique — holding the inside": get the inside first, move nobody.
  - "Penalty Kill": stay tight, hold the inside, block, clear, don't chase.
- **zone_entries**, "The defender's version of the offside rule": make the entry uncontrolled; on a delayed offside, head up.
- **faceoffs:**
  - "What actually to do": practise drill-first.
  - "The icing exception": the free post-icing violation, scoped by book.
- **game_management**: the instruction now opens the paragraph — "On every hit, ⚠️ **keep it off their head.**" The amber moves from the Hockey Canada scope clause onto the instruction.
- **passing_and_receiving**, "Area pass": "the safe option in the neutral zone" now carries its own limit, "only if your teammate has a genuine speed or angle advantage". The matching facts Read line gets the same limit, because facts lines are heard alone.
- **body_contact_and_battles:**
  - "The stick lift"
  - "A body check is the last step of a progression"
  - "The habits that keep you out of the box"

Also, the pre-existing special_teams bold sentence "**A shorthanded team cannot be called for icing.**" was scoped. It now reads: "**Where your book grants it, a team below the opponent's strength cannot be called for icing — except, under the NHL and PWHL, when a major expires with nobody in the box.**"

## Defects found by review, all repaired
**Rules-verifier**

- **FIX-cheaper, faceoffs icing exception (PWHL).** PWHL 78.4 reads "any subsequent violation will result in a bench minor" and omits NHL/IIHF 76.4's "by that team". Read literally, your first violation after their centre's free one can already be the penalised one. The lead had told PWHL readers to cheat expecting it to be free. Now: "Under the PWHL, assume one free violation for the whole draw". The coordinator carried the same claim to facts line ~:820 ("The warning is per team…" is now "Under the NHL and IIHF … the PWHL rule does not say so") and to ~:992 (the North American check list now includes the PWHL). A narrow rules re-check confirmed both.
- **WORDING, goaltender "Shootouts".** "A shootout attempt follows the penalty-shot rules" was unscoped. The NHL, IIHF, PWHL and USA Hockey (638(c)) cross-refer; Hockey Canada has no shootout rule and CARHA does not cross-refer. It is now scoped, with "play it the same way" for the two silent books.
- **WORDING, body_contact "The stick lift".** "A pass arriving" licensed a lift before the pass arrives, which IIHF 56.1 defines as interference ("receiving a pass"). The rules re-check then found:
  - "as a shot reaches their blade" was the wrong moment, because a shot reaches a screener's blade only when it is tipped;
  - the safety suggestion "never on a player bent low" contradicted :356 and :1771 on the same page.

  The final wording: "Lift a player's stick only as they are about to play the puck, or as a pass or shot arrives — … and let go at once; on a player bent low over the puck, take it low under the shaft or leave it, because a miss goes into the face."
- **WORDING, zone_entries.** "Any protection lapses" had no referent heard alone. The first repair, "the whistle that protects a puck carrier stops covering you…", was BLOCKED by the commit gate: it asserted a protecting whistle in every book, but Hockey Canada 6.11(b) and CARHA 72(e) write none, which overstates a Hockey Canada or CARHA reader's safety. It now reads "where your book has a whistle that protects the puck carrier, it stops covering you the instant you pass". A narrow rules re-read confirmed it: NHL, IIHF and PWHL 83.3/85.3 and USA Hockey 630(d) name the carrier; Hockey Canada and CARHA write nothing.

**Safety-reviewer**

- **R1, "The habits that keep you out of the box".** "Hands off their cage" implied hands elsewhere were fine. After the rules re-check (a bare "check with your trunk" read as a permission where checking is barred, and it dropped elbows and stick), it now reads "where checking is allowed, check with your trunk — never your hands, forearms, elbows or stick; never put a hand on their cage". A narrow safety re-read then had it set as a semicolon list (words unchanged), so that "where checking is allowed" cannot attach to the wrong clause when heard.
- **R2, stick lift.** The face hazard was missing; repaired as above.
- **R3, stick lift.** "Pass or shot" added; repaired as above.
- **R4, special_teams.** The flat "A shorthanded team cannot be called for icing" was scoped (above).

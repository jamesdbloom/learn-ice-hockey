## What changed and why
1. **bcb facts :1275 (screener).** Added "In a CARHA adult league no rule lets an attacker hold ice, so avert contact". CARHA 66(a) Note 2 grants "stand their ground" to defenders only, and 49(a) prices "does not avert body contact". A flattened sweep of CARHA 2026 (and 2020) for grant language found nothing else.
2. **bcb :947.** USA Hockey had been quoted with the conditional moving-ice grant introduced by "only" ("as long as … foot speed"). It now quotes the stand-your-ground bullet (usah.txt:380-381), scoped "for non-checking play": that bullet sits under the Preface's Competitive Contact Category, and the scope was added by the coordinator after the rules review. The NHL/IIHF 56.1 grant is now a paraphrase, because the NHL text says "he" and the old quote matched only the IIHF wording.
3. **faceoffs :1155 and :1330.** "the In-House Rules defer substitutions to 'the Playing Rules'" quoted a clause scoped to NIHL Playoff Final overtime (eiha_inhouse_2026-27.txt:557-566). :1155 now reads "no British rule book mentions a broken skate", verified flattened across every British source on disk. :1330 now carries the scope.
4. **rules_primer :329.** "names no broken skate" became "No Situation grants it — Situation 9.10's lost blade answers only whether play is stopped". The "answers only whether play is stopped" wording is the safety reviewer's, applied by the coordinator.
5. **Drill 20 (owner's source).** Hockey Tutorial, "Master The Elite Mohawk Turn", 13:30-14:01, read from the captions: "add a shoulder motion jumping from skate to skate … more power output … more weight into the blade". The switch is now "shoulders turn, then jump skate to skate" instead of two steps with both feet on the ice. The card asks the owner whether the coach's "more backwards than forward" weight shift belongs in this drill. (The Drill 7 note tidy shipped earlier, in ea32259.) Drill 20 has had no safety review; it needs one before the drills are published.

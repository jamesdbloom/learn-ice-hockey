## Review

The sweep edits (51 lines, then the review fixes) were reviewed by a rules-verifier on every `+` line, and by a safety-reviewer with md_to_speech renders of team_play, uk_rules, mental_game and time_and_space.

**Findings:**
- **1 MAJOR, raised by both reviewers.** team_play's new post-game fight bullet gave the PWHL's in-game "may be ejected" (46.1) and left out the NHL. NHL and PWHL 46.9 ("other than during the periods of the game … shall") are mandatory. Fixed.
- **5 MINORs, all fixed:**
  - KT12 helmet scope (USAH 615(c): your own "prior to", his "prior to or during");
  - the glossary's CARHA injury limb;
  - the uk_rules "them" pointer;
  - puck_support "forceful" scoped to the boards (Casebook 608 Situation 1 limb 1). The author declined the suggested "boards-facing" because it would make a minimal pinch eject;
  - time_and_space way-back, "can still be penalised".

Two authors declined a coordinator suggestion because the source said otherwise: time_and_space "can" rather than "is", and puck_support "hard … at the boards".

## What this method could not have found

- **Claim shapes outside the ten.** Every author swept by the brief's claim shapes and by keyword. A defect of another shape, or one worded without the trigger terms, would pass.
- **Layers built on an old answer.** Neither reviewer re-read the unchanged layers of each file for sentences now contradicted by the new text. The post-game 46.9 finding came only from reading one bullet in context.
- **Read-aloud for most documents.** Only 4 of the 13 edited documents were rendered.

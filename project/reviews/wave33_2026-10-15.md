## Coordinator rulings and lessons this wave

- **CARHA empty-net awarded goal.** 58(c) Note 1 awards the goal flat but cites the conditioned 36(b). Where a book contradicts itself the conservative reading ships, so the corpus plays to 58(c).
- **Trapezoid in Britain.** Nothing the Elite League publishes switches it off, and England Ice Hockey's R&R 22.3 calls it "not currently enforced" in its own competitions. Goalies play as though it binds; attackers in any British competition plan for the goalie to come out. Whether 22.3 reaches an EIHL fixture stays unsettled, as its owner says.
- **The brief was the defect, again.** The first standard trapezoid wording was the coordinator's, and two agents followed it into about 18 sites before a reviewer compared it with its owner. A standard wording in a brief is a claim, and it needs checking against the owner document before it is sent to ten agents.
- **The per-batch safety reviews were not enough on their own.** The facts layer and the summary layers had the most defects, and the dimension-specific reviewers found them. They now run on every wave.

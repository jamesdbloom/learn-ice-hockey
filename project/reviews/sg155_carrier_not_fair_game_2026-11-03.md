# SG155: the puck carrier is not fair game — holding counterweights beside "not in possession" interference limits (3 November 2026)

**Files:** content/hockey-iq/playing_without_the_puck.md; content/systems/defending_the_rush.md; content/systems/defensive_zone_coverage.md; content/technique/puck_handling.md; content/positions/winger.md; project/plans/OPEN_ITEMS.md

## Source
The SG154 follow-on row: interference reaches only an opponent not in possession (Hockey Canada 8.3, CARHA 66(a)(1), NHL/IIHF 56), and a page that states that next to advice about impeding or shadowing an opponent can be heard as clearing the puck carrier. Holding reaches the carrier in every book (NHL and IIHF 54.1, PWHL 55.1, Hockey Canada 8.1, CARHA Glossary with 63, USA Hockey 622 with no possession condition). A census of "not in possession" sites sent one agent per page group; sites whose subject is a non-carrier (a screener, a trailer, a goalie, a forechecker) were left alone.

## What changed
- playing_without_the_puck: :273 "In every book, once your man has the puck you still may not hold them"; facts :256 "Lay your stick across anyone, puck or no puck — that is hooking or holding … on the carrier, play the puck or lift their stick well below the hands" (was a stick-on-a-puckless-man line that could be heard as permitting a stick across the carrier, and the first repair said "play their stick" with no hands limit); Key focus :14 "never across anyone".
- defending_the_rush :350: after CARHA 66(a)(1), "That does not make the carrier fair game: take the ice, never hold the player", with each book's holding tariff.
- defensive_zone_coverage :203: a CM bullet that named only IIHF women's 101.1 for a pin now adds "In every book a pin with your arms can be called holding, puck or no puck" (USA Hockey 622 Note names the arm pin).
- puck_handling :499 facts and :521 body: the muddled "interference concerns a player who does not have the puck, which is not you" becomes "a free hand that grabs or restrains is holding, not interference, whoever has the puck" (NHL 56.1 "must be penalized as holding").
- winger :775 the same counterweight with each book's tariff (Hockey Canada a minor, a major and game misconduct if it injures; CARHA a minor or a major that ends your game, mandatory if it injures); facts :755 "take the puck, take the ice or angle them off it, never hold them"; :908 trailer cites 8.1 and the CARHA Glossary with 63.

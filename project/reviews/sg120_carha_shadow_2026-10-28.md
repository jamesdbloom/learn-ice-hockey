## What changed and why
CARHA 66(a) Note 2 (carha_2026.txt:3083-3086) says "the defending players are entitled to 'stand their ground' or 'shadow' the attacking players". Body Contact and Battles said three times that CARHA grants "the standing half only". That understated the grant: shadowing is a moving grant, so CARHA allows more than standing still. The three sentences now read:
- **:234 (facts, Rule:):** "NHL and IIHF 56.1 and PWHL 57.1 allow a player the ice they are standing on; USA Hockey's Preface (Competitive Contact Category) says 'entitled to the ice they occupy'. - **:949 (body):** "CARHA gives only defending players the right to 'stand their ground' or 'shadow' attackers, and Hockey Canada writes no such sentence"
- **:1413 (body):** "CARHA lets only defending players 'stand their ground' or 'shadow' attackers (66(a) Note 2), with 49(a) still pricing…"

A search of content/ for "standing half" now returns nothing.

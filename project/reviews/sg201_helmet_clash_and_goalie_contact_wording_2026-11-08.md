## What changed

### Head contact at the faceoff (center, faceoffs)
**Before.** Both pages priced a helmet clash at the dot only as the pre-drop faceoff minor. The minor is mandatory under USA Hockey 613(e), Hockey Canada 6.2(c) and CARHA 57(b). Leading with the helmet on purpose was never priced at all (cheaper).

**faceoffs.md now prices the clash at every layer:**
- **Facts:** five lines.
  - The pre-drop minor: discretionary under NHL and IIHF 76.7, mandatory under USA Hockey 613(e), Hockey Canada 6.2(c) and CARHA 57(b). Head contact can raise it.
  - USA Hockey 620: beyond pure accident, a minor plus a misconduct, or a major and game misconduct if intentional or careless, and a match for reckless endangerment. A purely accidental clash still costs the 613(e) minor.
  - Hockey Canada minor and female hockey, 7.6:
    - a minor, a double minor if intentional, a game ejection for a third (7.6(a));
    - a major and game misconduct for violence, mandatory if an accidental one injures (7.6(b));
    - a match if defenceless or meant to injure (7.6(c)).
  - Hockey Canada Junior and Senior, 7.7: a minimal clash is called as roughing or another penalty; moderate, a minor plus a misconduct; highly violent, a major and game misconduct; a match.
  - Head-butting: a major and game misconduct under NHL 47.3, PWHL 47.2 and USA Hockey 619(a); up to one under IIHF 47.3; a match under CARHA 48(b); at least a double minor under Hockey Canada 7.1, whose Interpretation 1 gives helmet-to-helmet as its example. When it injures: a match under NHL 47.4, PWHL 47.3, Hockey Canada 7.1(c) and CARHA 48(b), and USA Hockey 619(b) may add one.
- **Body:** the "What it costs" paragraph is split three ways.
  - The IIHF violation and ejection, ending on the mandatory minor.
  - "Keep your head off theirs, because head contact can raise that minor", with the USA Hockey, Hockey Canada and CARHA 50(a)–(c) ladders.
  - "Never lead with your helmet", with each book's head-butting price.
- **Common Mistakes and KT3:** the mandatory minor, which head contact can raise, and the head-butting limb.

**center.md:**
- **New paragraph after :563.** It covers:
  - minor and female hockey: a clash is head contact, which "supersedes any other rules";
  - the 7.6 ladder, including "must if an accidental one injures";
  - Junior and Senior: minimal (roughing), moderate (minor plus misconduct), highly violent (a major and game misconduct, mandatory);
  - 7.1 head-butting.
- **Common Mistakes :817.**
  - Head-butting in every book: a major and game misconduct (NHL, PWHL, USA Hockey); a match (CARHA); a double minor up to a major and game misconduct (IIHF); at least a double minor (Hockey Canada).
  - USA Hockey 620 beyond pure accident.
  - Every book but the IIHF can make a head-butt a match.
- **Facts :529.** A clash "can be Head Contact under Hockey Canada".

### Hockey Canada "head or neck", and CARHA's crease cross-check (winger)
- **Head contact reaches "head, face, or neck".** This is the 7.6/7.7 preambles' wording. Facts :751 (299/300), :553, :560 and :779 now say "head or neck" where they said "head".
- **CARHA 54(c) added at :536 and CM :799.** It makes cross-checking a goalkeeper in the crease a mandatory major and game misconduct at any height (carha_2026.txt:2637-2640).

### USA Hockey's goal test (special_teams, shooting, body_contact, center, offensive_zone_play, playing_without_the_puck)
- **"Contact that interferes" became "interfering with the goalie" for USA Hockey.** 617(c)(4)'s own word is "interfering with the goalkeeper", and Casebook 625 Situations 9 and 13 do not require contact. CARHA keeps "contact that interferes", because 66(b) says "has physically interfered". Penalty limbs are unchanged, since 625(a)(8) requires contact.
- **center's "contact or not" was removed.** It went beyond any USA Hockey text and contradicted sibling pages.

### Goalie contact, other limbs (offensive_zone_play, playing_without_the_puck)
- **CARHA 66(b) fouled-attacker limbs.** These are at offensive_zone_play facts :688 and body :708, and playing_without_the_puck :732.
  - When the attacker's team has the puck and a defender fouled them: a minor, and the goal disallowed.
  - An attacker not carrying the puck who is tripped or caused to fall: a minor wherever the goalie is.
- **playing_without_the_puck facts :712.** Each CARHA tier is now credited to the rule that supplies it: 66(b) for the impeding minor, the 52(b) Note for unnecessary contact (minor or major), 66(b) for deliberate contact, 30(a) and 66(e).
- **offensive_zone_play facts :595.** It now reads: NHL 69.4 penalises unnecessary goalie contact outside the crease "in every case", with incidental contact allowed only as the goalie plays the puck; Hockey Canada and CARHA have no play-the-puck allowance; Hockey Canada 8.5 penalises any failure to make that effort.
- **offensive_zone_play :620.** Reordered so the condition comes first. It also has the Hockey Canada 8.5 "may occur" / "in all circumstances" duty.

## Defects found by review
1. **offensive_zone_play :595, permissive (blocked by both goalie reviewers).** The first pass read "under Hockey Canada 8.5 an effort to avoid it saves the penalty". That stated the converse of the book's duty. The next pass, "write none", lost its antecedent: heard alone it said three books have no goalie-contact rule. The final pass dropped USA Hockey, because Casebook 607 Situation 5 grants a possession-based permission. That permission is not voiced on this page, the same decision as special_teams in SG199.
2. **faceoffs.** After the first repair, the Hockey Canada clash was still priced as the faceoff minor, while center's new paragraph pointed to faceoffs for the detail (blocking). The next repair dropped the mandatory minor from KT3 and gave Hockey Canada minor and female hockey a misconduct that 7.6(d) bars (blocking). The final reads found the missing match options: USA Hockey 620(c) and 619(b), NHL 47.4, PWHL 47.3, CARHA 50(c). Also too broad: "mandatory if it injures" (now "an accidental one") and "a match if … careless" (the match is for reckless endangerment).
3. **center.** "Violence can make it a major and game misconduct in any division" read the Junior and Senior tier as discretionary, but 7.7(b) says "will be assessed" (blocking). Head-butting was priced for Hockey Canada only. "Contact or not" was an over-reach.
4. **faceoffs SG201b, a premise correction.** The page never stated Hockey Canada's 7.6/7.7 ladder at all. The defect was in the helmet-to-helmet minor.

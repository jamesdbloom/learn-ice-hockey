3. **Fixes from review** (fix authors X and Y, from a merged brief; the coordinator resolved four reviewer disagreements):
   - **puck_handling small-area control (safety BLOCKING, all three layers, two of them pre-existing).** "Turn away from" arriving pressure told a player to show their back to an oncoming checker. It now reads: turn side-on, hip and backside between it and the puck, never your back to anyone coming at you.
   - **body_contact bracing (rules FIX-harsher, every layer of the section).** "In women's hockey the move is itself an illegal hit" was too narrow. Stepping into a hit is a penalty wherever checking is barred or restricted: IIHF 101.1 and PWHL 52.1 in women's hockey; Hockey Canada 7.3(a) at U13 and below and in female hockey; USA Hockey 604(c) at 12U, girls', women's and non-check adult. Under CARHA 49(a), avoid the contact altogether. A new Rule line carries the per-book list.
     - "Barred or restricted" is the fix author's correction of the sketch: IIHF and PWHL women's play restricts checking rather than barring it.
     - The narrow re-check then restored the charging limb the fix had dropped from the lead: "in any league, building speed into a collision is what the charging rules reach".
   - **body_contact tripping.** The lead is now rule-free. The body and Rule line add Hockey Canada 8.6 Interpretations 1–2 and the CARHA 86(a)/(b) Notes. Those excuse winning the puck in the same action, or a falling or sliding player who touches the puck first. The fix author refused the sketch's "only", which would have been a false exclusivity.
   - **body_contact press-down (content MAJOR, every layer).** The lead and the section said "pin it while a teammate arrives". That contradicted the document's own Common Mistakes and Key Takeaway 10 ("press… as the puck arrives, then let go; a press you hold can be called holding"). Reconciled throughout, plus "or high on their shaft" (USA Hockey Casebook, Standard of Play).
   - **defensive_zone_coverage Assignments (content MAJOR plus safety).** The lead had borrowed "no bump on a player without the puck" and dropped "or at all where checking is barred". The clause was removed; the next lead, in the same chunk, carries the full limit.
   - **zone_entries pick limit, at five sites (two found beyond the brief).** "Never cut across into their path, or stop, stand or step into them". This follows NHL 56.1, which defines a pick as moving into an opponent's path without body position. The drop pass gains the paint stop, and the interference reason is now that the defender has no puck.
   - **puck_handling multiple defenders.** "Under CARHA, avert the contact rather than take it", in the Options line and the bullet.
   - **center facts line (pre-existing place-versus-competition scope).** "Across the Home Countries there is no stick-down order" was false voiced alone for an Elite League centre. It now reads "Under IHUK's In-House Rules, not the Elite League".
   - **Other fixes:**
     - defender trapezoid keeps "though not freeze it out there";
     - shooting gains a Never line on the goalie's head;
     - an unlabelled "often beats a stronger player" and a study's conclusion stated as fact were removed;
     - "at even strength" scoping in offensive_zone_play;

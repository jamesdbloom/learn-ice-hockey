## What changed
Sites are listed by claim.

- **defending_the_rush :354, :434, CM :1018: "into the boards or the net".**
  - The defenceless-player mandatory match now covers the net as well as the boards.
  - Sources: HC 7.5(c) *"into the boards or goal frame"*; CARHA 53(b) *"into the boards or goal net"*.
  - The facts lines already said "goal frame", and KT9 "or net".
- **team_play_and_culture Key focus :26, body :38, KT6 :715: "can … in every book".**
  - Disputing a call can be penalised in every book. HEAD said "most books".
  - NHL, PWHL and IIHF 39.2 and USA Hockey 601(a)(1) say "shall"; CARHA 46(a) says "must"; Hockey Canada 11.1(i) is discretionary and needs "an unsportsmanlike manner".
- **mental_game :575, CM :694, KT11 :752.**
  - CM and KT11: "can earn" and "can hand".
  - :575: USA Hockey swearing at anyone is a misconduct before or during the game (601(c)(2)) and a game misconduct after it (601(d)(10)).
  - :575: other abuse that comes with disputing a call starts at the minor and climbs if you keep at it (Casebook 601, Situations 3 and 9: *"This sequence must be followed in every instance"*).
- **rules_primer :427: Hockey Canada 7.7.**
  - Moderate violence is a minor plus a misconduct, so a second such hit is a game misconduct (7.7(a), 4.7(c)).
  - New: a stick, hand, forearm, shoulder or elbow raised or driven into the head never drops to 9.5's bare minor; neither does a blindside or late hit to the head, or a leaping hit or a hit on a player down that reaches the head or neck (Interpretation 1 to 7.7(a), *"Notwithstanding degree of violence … minimum of a Minor penalty and a Misconduct Penalty"*). This gap pre-dates the wave and stated the act more cheaply than the book.
- **rules_primer :568 and :1028.**
  - After the game, swearing is a game misconduct: USA Hockey 601(d)(10), NHL and PWHL 39.5(ii) and 75.5(v)/77.5(v), IIHF 39.5(II) and 75.5(V).
  - :1028: obscene or profane language is a misconduct under USA Hockey 601(c)(2) and CARHA 46(b)(1) with no condition attached, and Hockey Canada does the same for personal abuse (11.2(d)). Under USA Hockey, other abuse that comes with arguing a call follows the Situation 3 ladder.
- **body_contact_and_battles :832: Junior and Senior.**
  - A 7.7 head-contact minor carries a misconduct, so a second one is a game misconduct (7.7(a)/(d), 4.7(c), reported under 4.8(a)).
  - The 7.6 three-minor Game Ejection is now scoped to minor and female hockey. HEAD said 7.7 "neither names it".
- **center KT6 :888, switching_positions KT8 :636.**
  - Old: "USA Hockey does [make it a match] for any reckless one".
  - New: "under USA Hockey, plan on a match and a likely multi-game ban for any reckless one".
  - Why: 602(a) *"shall"* conflicts with 608(b)'s major plus game misconduct, and 608(c) needs excessive force. The Standardized Discipline Policy sets the ban levels.
- **special_teams facts :1075, body :1084, CM :1195; body_contact :1314: goalie contact.** Corrected in both directions.
  - **Goal test, by book:**
    - Hockey Canada (Interpretation 1 to 8.5(a)) takes the goal for any contact the attacker starts, anywhere.
    - USA Hockey 617(c)(4) takes it for *"interfering with the goalkeeper"*.
    - CARHA 66(b) takes it where the attacker *"has physically interfered with the goalkeeper"*.
    - The NHL, IIHF and PWHL (69.1/71.1) take it for deliberate contact in or out of the crease, and for planting in the crease in the goalie's way or sightline (Table 14 5D).
  - **Penalty:** leaning on the goalie as you screen is a penalty in every book.
    - Sources: NHL and IIHF 69.2, PWHL 71.2, Hockey Canada 8.5 *"anywhere on the ice"*, CARHA 66(b) *"whether in or out of the goal crease"*, USA Hockey 607(d) Note 1 and 625(a)(8).
    - A net-front screener is always inside USA Hockey's privileged area (607(d) Note 2), so Casebook 607 Situation 5's possession limb does not apply to them.
  - **The wave's first draft was wrong in two ways:**
    - It grouped CARHA with Hockey Canada ("contact you start costs it anywhere"). That overstates CARHA, the harsher direction, and contradicted goaltender.md :544.
    - It gave USA Hockey only "interfering", with no penalty limb, which a listener could take as making light contact free.
  - **The first repair overreached:** it wrote "in the crease or out" across all six books. That gave a goalie false protection and contradicted Situation 5 and goaltender.md, so it was narrowed to the screen.

## Wrong premises found
1. **The first-pass CARHA grouping.** CARHA's goal test is interference, as in USA Hockey. Only Hockey Canada voids the goal for any contact.
2. **"USA Hockey makes profanity a misconduct only when you are not disputing a call".** This first-pass carve-out was cheaper than the book. 601(c)(2) has no carve-out (Casebook 601 Sit. 1); the ladder in Situations 3 and 9 applies to abuse.
3. **"In every book, leaning on or shoving the goalie is a penalty, in the crease or out".** This was the first repair's own overreach, and false for USA Hockey outside the privileged area (Casebook 607 Sit. 5).
4. **The brief said nine content files.** The wave touched eight.

## What changed and why
On Windows, the 🇬🇧 emoji renders as the letters "GB" glued to the next word. Mid-sentence it read "…next game. GB The IIHF writes…" (101 places on 19 pages, found in the SG73 site review). The owner chose option (a) on 25 October: keep the flag only where it opens a paragraph, list item, blockquote line, table cell or facts value, and say it in words everywhere else.

Four agents, each owning disjoint files, removed or reworded every mid-prose flag; 86 remain, all opening a block. Almost every change is a pure deletion, because the sentence already named Britain, the UK, the Home Countries, a British competition or the British reader. Nothing was widened from a competition to "Britain". The convention is now in the style guide.

### Commit gate 1: BLOCK, then a fresh reader

The first commit gate blocked on NZ :394. The fix that restored the full HC 7.4(c) quotation had reached :345 but not :394. :394 was restored, and the gate's note on faceoffs :952 ("the same exception" was loose) was applied. A `rules-verifier` then read both lines as staged and found them CLEAR. It raised two optional points, recorded in the plan:

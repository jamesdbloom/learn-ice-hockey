# Waves 63–64 (17 October 2026): USA Hockey 604(e) applies to reckless endangerment, not to a plain check

## The claim

The corpus read USA Hockey 604(e) as letting a match penalty reach a plain body check in a non-checking game. Two documents said so deliberately: "named for reckless endangerment, but the act it is assessed for is the plain body check".

A final review of the puck_handling Style B script ruled that reading wrong. A rules-verifier then confirmed the ruling against the sources:

- **The rule itself.** usah.txt 604(e) reads "A match penalty **for reckless endangerment** may also be assessed for body checking in a Competitive Contact category or non-check Adult classification". That is the same construction as every sibling "may also be assessed" limb.
- **The Casebook.** Casebook 604 Situation 3 treats (d) and (e) as one reckless tier: "Rule Reference 604(d and e)".
- **The Glossary** defines reckless endangerment.
- **Three places frame (e) as an alternative to (d):** the Disabled Hockey section, the Preface and the Casebook all say "major plus game misconduct **or** match".

The error ran in the harsh direction.

The correct pricing in a USA Hockey non-checking game:
- A plain check is a minor (604(c)).
- A reckless check is a major plus a game misconduct, or a match (604(d)/(e)). Plan on the match (602(a)).

Every "plan on a match (602(a))" pairing is kept.

### Commit gate, safety pass and the :503 repair

The first commit-gate run BLOCKED on C6: the fix lowers a stated penalty and no safety-reviewer had seen it. The accuracy of the change was confirmed. The safety pass that followed found no blocker and one MAJOR. rules_primer :503's "a plain check there is a minor" never said what makes a check reckless, so a non-check reader who throws a hard, no-puck check, or hits a player already battling for the puck, would expect two minutes. ## What this method could not have found

- **A plain-check-ejects claim worded without "604", "plain check", "match" or "end your game".** For example: "thrown out for any bump".
- **USA Hockey's other routes.** rules_primer :503 now names the Declaration of Player Safety triggers and boarding (603(a)). It does not name checking from behind, which is a plan row.
- **Paraphrases elsewhere.** Other documents may carry an unglossed plain-versus-reckless split. Only rules_primer and body_contact were checked for it.
- **Stacking.** Whether 603 and 604 are both assessed for one check in a non-check game; no text was found either way.

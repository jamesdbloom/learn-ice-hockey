# W632: bringing the corpus to the PWHL 2026-27 rulebook (23 November 2026)

Following the W630 D source-verification, `sources/pwhl_rules_2026-27.txt` is the operative PWHL book. Four agents, each owning a disjoint set of about 25 pages, propagated its changes. The brief was `scratchpad/w632_common.md`. Each group then got a rules-verifier or a safety-reviewer, and every finding was applied.

## What changed

**52.1 Body Checking, rewritten.**
- The old limbs are gone: opposite-directional force, the skating lane, "extends an arm, shoulder, or hip", "obliged to skate around", the avoid-contact duty, and "any step or glide… at least a minor".
- The new illegal limbs are (b)(i) a blindside check on a defenceless opponent, (b)(ii) launching, driving an arm or leaving the feet, and (b)(iii) deliberately stepping in with excessive force.
- 52.5's game misconduct now needs an injury.
- bcb :189, the corpus's main statement, now quotes the new text, and all quotations were verified verbatim.
- Most edits make lines cheaper, because the corpus was harsher than the new book. The new limbs were added only where a page prices PWHL checking.

**82 High-sticking the puck, reversed.**
- Batting above the shoulders "is permitted", and play goes on.
- A goal off a high stick counts, except on a penalty shot or shootout, or where 61.1 applies (82.2, 15.1 Note).
- passing :439's false "prohibited in all six books" now reads "five books; the PWHL now permits it".

**The trapezoid.**
- 65.2(viii) is deleted, and 1.8 says there is none.
- About 40 sites carried the "the book contradicts itself, play as if it binds" advice. Each now says the PWHL has no trapezoid (1.8, 27.8), which is shorter everywhere.
- ⚠️ paragraphs whose only hazard was the stale advice now carry 65.2(i)'s freeze-outside-the-crease minor.
- The `dump-and-the-trapezoid` caption was fixed.
- Items (i)–(vii) of 65.2 keep their letters, so no other 65.2 citation moved.

**9.10 Neck Protector, new and mandatory.** It was added to equipment, uk_rules :184 and goaltender :1461. equipment carries the replace-before-you-play limb.

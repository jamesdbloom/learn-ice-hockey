## What changed and why
- **team_play :288.** The line said 615(e) is a minor "for not going straight to the bench when told during an altercation". That is half the rule, and it is wrong for a goalie, who stands in the handshake line this section covers. usah.txt 615(e): "fails to move immediately and directly to their players' bench (or goal crease for a goalkeeper) when instructed to do so by the Referee during an altercation". The line now reads "your bench — or your crease, if you are the goalie". Its 615(a) clause, about dropping your stick or gloves when you were not in the original altercation, is unchanged; the verifier checked it against the rule and it is faithful.
- **rules_primer :878 and goaltender :1532 (PWHL 14.1).** pwhl_rules.txt 14.1 reads "No delay shall be permitted… Unless at the discretion of the Referee, a fifteen (15) second delay will be permitted for quick adjustments". The PWHL minor in 65.2(x) and 67.2 repeats "No delay" with no window.
  - **First edit.** Both sites were changed to "allows fifteen seconds … unless the referee decides otherwise", the literal plain reading. The safety-reviewer returned FIX. That wording is permissive: it makes the window sound like a right, and the penalty rule does not repeat it. Heard aloud, it also contradicted the "no delay" stated just before it.
  - **What ships.** "14.1 starts from no delay… the PWHL's 14.1 adds a fifteen-second window for a quick fix such as a pad strap, at the referee's discretion, though / but its minor (65.2) repeats "No delay"". goaltender :1532 adds "so do not count on it".
  - Both sites keep "expect the backup to go in".

### The Hockey Canada crease-cover ruling

The case: a player inside their own crease covers a puck lying outside it. Wave 51 (DZC) and wave 52 (game_management) had moved Hockey Canada to the puck reading, giving a minor. The wave-52 safety review blocked this as permissive:

- 4.11(a)(v) ("while the puck is in the goal crease") is sufficient for the shot. It does not cancel 10.2(a)(v) ("when the infraction occurs inside the offending team's goal crease").
- Interpretation 5 has the player outside the crease too, so it cannot settle the case.

The coordinator ruled the conservative reading, recorded in the plan:

- **Hockey Canada adds a where-you-are test to the puck test.** Price the shot, or with the net empty the goal (10.2(a)(vi); 4.12(a) for a puck in the crease).
- **Five books decide by the puck:** NHL and IIHF 63.6, PWHL 65.6, USAH Casebook 614 Sit. 1, CARHA 58(c).

One claim brief was dispatched to every carrier: game_management, defensive_zone_coverage, defending_the_rush, special_teams, puck_handling, passing_and_receiving, risk_management and faceoffs. A `rules-verifier` then searched content/ by the act and ran a layer test on the seven files.

The final review found that "Hockey Canada is the exception" and "where you are" could be heard as *replacing* the puck test. Those sites now say Hockey Canada "adds" the test.

Three carriers are held:
- center.md :547 and rules_primer.md :534: held for NotebookLM batch 1.
- defender.md :207: held for Style B.

These, and body_contact_and_battles.md :1102, are plan rows.

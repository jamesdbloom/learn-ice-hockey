The shooting script author raised one omission, item (f): does USA Hockey's mandatory major and ejection for careless head or face contact, including a goalie's mask, need to appear in the slap-shot or high-stick layers? It was examined and answered NO CHANGE:
- Body :544 already carries USA Hockey 620(b) ("intentionally or carelessly contacts"), including a careless hit to a goaltender's mask.
- USA Hockey Casebook Rule 621 Situation 1 (usah_casebook.txt:13893-13906) puts stick-to-head contact under Head Contact only "during the process of delivering a check". Otherwise it is high-sticking, which is Rule 621. So a shooter's wind-up or follow-through falls under 621, not 620.
- 621's reckless tier is already carried at :181.

**The time-out rule behind W523(2):**
- The NHL, IIHF and PWHL grant none after a faceoff violation (87.1; PWHL 89.1).
- USA Hockey closes the window at the end of the line change (636(f); Casebook 636 Sit. 11).
- Hockey Canada closes it when the official whistles to start the faceoff (6.17(e)).
- CARHA writes no time-out at all. This is a searched negative, taken across the whole file.

## Agents B and C: the British third man

**Agent B's ruling:**
- **EIHL.** Its Casebook Preamble adopts the 2026/27 IIHF book and Situation Handbook. Its Rule 46 has no third-player clause, so IIHF 46.14 governs. Casebook 46.2 separately ejects any fighting major taken after the original altercation started.
- **In-House competitions.** The edition is unresolved between 2025/26 46.7 and 2026/27 46.14 (`sources/README.md` :35). No British document departs from either.
- **IHUK DOPS.** It lists "3rd Man-in … Up to 1" for clubs in England and Wales.
- **The permissive defect.** uk_rules :308 repeated the 2025/26 rule text's exemption ("except when a game misconduct penalty is being imposed in the original altercation"). It omitted that the IIHF's own Situation Handbook gives the third player the game misconduct in a plain mutual fight (Situations 46.1, 46.11) and in the boarding case (46.16).
- **The Handbooks.** The safety-reviewer confirmed that the 2026/27 Handbook keeps the same answers (:3634, :3763, :3820), so "in either edition" holds. Both Handbooks still cite "Rule 46.7", which in 2026/27 is fighting off the playing surface.

# Wave 71 (18 October 2026): "up to a mandatory major" corrected corpus-wide

## The claim

About 25 units across 11 documents priced Hockey Canada head contact (7.6; 7.7 in Junior/Senior) as "up to a mandatory major and game misconduct". The 7.6(b) major is mandatory only on injury or for a cross-check above the shoulders, and is otherwise discretionary on violence. The 7.7(b) major is mandatory at high violence, for a cross-check above the shoulders, or for a head injury off the glass, ice or boards. Moderate violence under 7.7 is a minor plus misconduct (7.7(a)). Many of the same units stated 7.5 flat as "a minor and game misconduct" and dropped 7.5(b)'s major (discretionary on violence, mandatory on injury).

## Models used

- Center :696, CM :736 and KT5, and winger :663 from wave 70 were the accepted forms.
- The long form is "anything from a minor up to a match — the major and game misconduct mandatory if he is hurt (minor and female hockey), if the hit is highly violent or injures his head on the glass, ice or boards (Junior and Senior), or for a cross-check above the shoulders, and a match if he could not protect himself…".
- Special Teams uses an equivalent "up to a major and game misconduct — mandatory for …" form.
- Facts lines carry compressed forms with the triggers scoped by rule.

## What this method could not have found

- **A Hockey Canada head price written without a rule number or "mandatory".** For example, "a five and a game".
- **The Junior/Senior minimal-violence route.** It goes through roughing or charging, which make the major mandatory on injury, so the model's "(minor and female hockey)" injury scope is half the answer (rules_primer reviewer, M1). This is a plan row.
- **Spoken order of the model.** It places each scope label after its condition, which is harder to follow aloud. Reordering would change every carrier, so it is a plan row.

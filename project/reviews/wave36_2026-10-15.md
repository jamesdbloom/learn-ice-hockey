# Wave 36 (15 October 2026)

## What ran

This wave applied two owner rulings of 15 October across the corpus:

- **Hockey Canada head contact goes through 7.6.**
  - A hit from behind that reaches the head, or that drives a player head-first into the boards, is head contact under Rule 7.6, or 7.7 in Junior and Senior. Only that price is stated.
  - The instruction still leads: never finish a check on a turned back.
  - The ruling was not extended to 8.5 (goaltender contact).
- **CARHA is relabelled to the 2026 book.** Every CARHA link and edition label now names the 2026 rule book, with "read in the 2020 printing; every rule cited here is unchanged in the 2026 book". The wave-35 research diffed the two books in full: only 75(a), 85(c), the Section Seven shoot-out sentence and Case Situation 85 changed, and only rules_primer cites any of them.

Twelve content agents each owned a disjoint file set and worked from one brief. The canonical ladder they applied was the wave-35 HC76 standard, re-checked against `sources/hc.txt` (the 10 September 2026 republication).

The review ran in stages. First came four reviewers: two safety reviewers by file group, a rules-verifier for cross-file consistency, and a facts / CARHA / non-negotiable-6 reviewer. Systems review C had run earlier in the wave. Next came a fix round driven by one canonical ladder (`_FIX.md`). Three final safety checks by file group followed. The re-fixes then got a last read, and that read's own findings were fixed too.

## Changes, by reason

- **Hockey Canada 7.6/7.7 carve-out (owner ruling).**
  - The full ladder now sits once per document, in the owner section, and leads with the instruction:
    - **7.6:** a minor if accidental, a double minor if intentional, and a third penalty ejects. A major and game misconduct at the referee's discretion on violence, mandatory on any injury or a cross-check above the shoulders. A match where the player could not protect himself, or on an attempt to injure.
    - **7.7:** minimal violence may be called as roughing, charging or another penalty. Moderate violence is a minor and a ten-minute misconduct. High violence, a cross-check above the shoulders, or a head injury off the glass, ice or boards is a major and game misconduct. Then the match.
  - Summary layers carry a short form with no floor: "head contact (Rule 7.6; 7.7 in Junior and Senior), up to a mandatory major and game misconduct, and a match if he could not protect himself".
  - Universal "no bare minor / ends your night" claims next to the carve-out are scoped to a hit that stays on the back.
  - 7.7(b)'s head-injury limb is stated as glass, ice and boards only. It is never applied to the goal frame.
- **CARHA relabel:** 36 trailer sites.
  - special_teams now cites 75(b), not "75".
  - DZC :850 dates its 2020 read and its 2026 re-check separately.
  - equipment adds the 2020-printing clause.
- **Non-negotiable 6:** extraction-file names and line locators were removed from Sources trailers in center, team_play, practice_and_development, breakouts, body_contact, winger, forechecking, shooting, ozp, risk_management, playing_without_the_puck, time_and_space, DZC, DTR, special_teams and equipment. Every disclosure and negative is kept, and the reviewers re-ran each negative whose method wording changed. Method narration that backs an honest negative ("whitespace-flattened", "layout extraction") remains in several trailers and is a plan row.
- **Found by review and repaired, beyond the ruling:**
  - **passing_and_receiving, permissive, three sites:** Hockey Canada Junior and Senior were said to have "no three-strikes provision". In fact 4.8(b)(i) ejects on three stick-infraction minors (9.1 to 9.5).
  - **body_contact facts (2):** a missed stick lift or follow-through to the head was priced at a 7.6(a) minor with no injury tier. Each was split into two lines that carry the tiers.
  - **rules_primer:**
    - "two minutes is not the answer anywhere else" was false (IIHF 48.2, HC 7.6(a)).
    - "620(a) and 7.6 are the same shape" was loose.
    - KT8 and CM :1047 gave the 7.6(a) floor without its tiers.
  - **puck_handling and special_teams:** both note that 10.2(a)(v) still words the crease penalty shot as a foul inside the crease.

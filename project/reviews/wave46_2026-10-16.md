# Wave 46 (16 October 2026): PWHL and CARHA left out of claims that name only some books

## What ran

Wave 41 checked every claim that counts the rulebooks, but only where a count word appears ("every book", "all six"). It said plainly that it could not catch a sentence that names only some books with no count word, such as "NHL-only", "NHL and IIHF only" or "the only book that differs", when the PWHL (whose rule numbers shift) or CARHA writes the same rule.

1. **Sweep.** One read-only `rules-verifier` split every document into sentences (defender excluded) and tested each one.
   - 730 sentences name only a subset of the books.
   - It read all 112 that use an exclusivity word, and all 174 in summary layers.
   - It found 11 exclusivity claims that the PWHL or CARHA contradicts. Two of them were permissive for a PWHL reader.
   - It also found five permissive or unclear CARHA omissions, and a lower tier.
   - About 700 sentences are incomplete but make no exclusive claim. Those are a plan row, not a defect.
2. **Fix round 1.** Five agents worked disjoint sets of files.
3. **Review.** One `rules-verifier` read every changed span word by word and found 0 BLOCKER, 1 MAJOR and 6 MINOR.
4. **Fix round 2.** One agent fixed all seven review findings plus one optional item. They are listed unit by unit below, and the commit gate later re-derived them.

## Why
Several units said a hit from behind (kept off the head) ends your game "in every book but USA Hockey", or listed the four books that eject, without saying in the same unit that USA Hockey ejects too in some cases. Under USA Hockey Casebook 608 Situation 1, a forceful check on a player standing along the boards *"must be called"* as a major plus game misconduct or a match, and so must a head-first or reckless one (608(b)). A ```facts line is voiced alone, so a limb carried only by the next line does not reach the listener. Heard alone, each such unit told a USA Hockey player the hit was a minor plus misconduct: the permissive direction. SG136 found the shape on forechecking; a read-only census found it on five more pages.

SG137 also closed two SG136 carry-overs: game_management's subjectless "if hurt" and dzc :209's length.

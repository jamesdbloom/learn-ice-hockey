- a coaching cue must never use a rule's own penalised verb;
- a scene keeps every mandatory tier that applies to it;

- USA Hockey 608(c) is a mandatory match;
- a reordered paragraph is new text.

- **New section.** A What it costs section (before Common Mistakes) with two subsections, A hit from behind and Contact with the goalie.
- **The goalie subsection** holds the wide entry's two bullets verbatim, plus a third bullet carrying the limbs only the old dump paragraph had.
- **Body scenes** keep the instruction and the scene tier, then one pointer: the delayed offside, wide entry, middle drive, numbers, dump, boards and chase.
- **Labels.** The icing label "What it costs." became "Check your book." (no content change).
- **KT10** is shortened. KT4 and the CM forecheck bullet were kept, as the census allowed.

**risk_management (W240).**
- **New section.** A What it costs section with three subsections: Leaving your feet: charging; Slew-footing; A hit from behind.
- **The §7 charging ladder** was not in the census but prices a foul, so it moved too. Its body label became "The price.".
- **Decision sections kept their prices** (fouling a breakaway, covering the puck in your own crease, clearing over the glass), because there the price is the decision.
- **Facts.** No facts block was consolidated.

**Fixes from review, risk_management:**
- **B1 (safety, BLOCKING).** The §7 scene had priced a reckless USA Hockey charge at a major plus game misconduct with no match. It now carries "plan on a match and a likely multi-game suspension there (602(a))" in the same chunk (017).
- **§7 injury tier.** British women's hockey ("any major ejects you"; junior "plan on it too"; In-House Rules 101 and 201, IIHF 201.1) is restored at the scene. "Mandatory in most books" is replaced, in the narrow re-check's wording, by "Injure someone and Hockey Canada and CARHA make it a major and a game misconduct, and the NHL, PWHL and Elite League do the same when a major injures the face or head".
- **Immediate-response scene.** The bare "(608(b))" pinned the "forceful check on a player standing along the boards" limb on a rule that lacks it. It now reads "(608(b); Casebook 608, Situation 1) — and if it is reckless, plan on the match and a likely multi-game suspension (602(a))".
- **Hockey Canada head clause.** "Head contact instead" pointed a head-first drive into the boards at the cheaper floor. F840 (the rules-verifier's text, 294/300), the scene and the What it costs lead now say "on the head it is head contact, a match if they cannot protect themselves, and a head-first drive into the boards takes the harsher of that and checking from behind". The head paragraph also names the match (7.6(c); 7.7(c) in Junior and Senior).
- **Slew-foot scene.** It now names the four books that eject at the floor.
- **"Leaving your feet" lead.** "Be on your skates at the moment of contact" was Hockey Canada's test without its scope; USA Hockey Casebook 607 Sit. 1 makes any jump while delivering a check a charge. It now reads "keep your skates on the ice through the hit, and hit only where your league allows checking".
- **Read aloud.** The pointers that sounded like broken clauses were reworded, and the 7.4(c) inline amber was restored.

**Fixes from review, zone_entries:**
- **B1 (safety, BLOCKING, pre-existing in every layer).** The Hockey Canada "off the head" carve-out had its counterweight in the body only. Facts :239 and :833 now say "(Hockey Canada: if off the head; on it, a match if defenceless)", and CM :1185 carries the same "harsher of" parenthesis as the body.
- **B2 (rules, BLOCKING, pre-existing).** :809 said USA Hockey "writes no such permission" for contact with a goalie outside the crease, which is false: Casebook 607 Situation 5 permits engaging a goalie who has the puck outside the privileged area. It contradicted goaltender.md. The page now leads with the limit: a check meant to "intimidate or punish" is charging, and so is avoidable contact once the goalie has lost the puck. It then gives the permission only where the game allows body checking (604(a): never at 12U and below, in any Girls' or Women's classification or in non-check adult play, or wherever your league or local governing body bars it).
- **:1164, the USA Hockey goalie floor.** The first fix scoped "never a bare minor" to the crease or privileged area. The narrow re-check found that wording made contact elsewhere sound cheaper, because 607(a) has no location limit. It now reads "never a bare minor — at least a minor plus a misconduct (607(a); in the crease or privileged area, 607(c) too)".
- **:1157.** "An opponent like any other" read as a permission and undersold the goalie rules. It now says the same tiers apply "too, at a minimum", that a goalie out of the crease is still not "fair game" in any book, and that Hockey Canada 8.5(b) makes a charge on one a mandatory major and game misconduct.
- **Named books.** "Most books" now names the NHL, PWHL and CARHA (:254, :851).
- **:361.** It now says "even accidental contact is interference" (607(d) Note 1) and restores the rebound-scramble limit beside the caption that states the permission.
- **:1155.** "costs your team's next game" became "suspends you for your team's next game" (404(b)).
- **:434.** The pointer read as broken aloud and was reworded.
- **602(a) restored at four scenes (commit gate, BLOCKING).** The first gate found that the delayed-offside (:254), wide-entry (:361), dump and boards (:811/:813) and chase (:851) scenes had each lost USA Hockey 602(a)'s mandatory match ("A match penalty shall be assessed to any player … who recklessly endangers") from their own chunk. HEAD carried it there. Each now says, scoped to reckless, "plan on a match and a likely multi-game suspension (602(a))", in chunks 019, 026, 050 and 052. :254 and :851 also got back the next-game cost: USA Hockey 404(b) and 403(b), and CARHA 32(d).
- **:809 completed.** The limit clause now quotes Casebook 607 Sit. 5 in full: *"[a] penalty should be assessed in every instance where unnecessary or avoidable contact is made with the goalkeeper – even when outside the privileged area"*. Then: "while they have the puck, a check meant to 'intimidate or punish' the goalie is charging, and so is any avoidable contact once they have lost the puck". The author's first draft added "even while they have the puck" to the avoidable-contact test. The narrow re-check found that the book applies that test only after possession is lost, and the coordinator applied the reviewer's verbatim fix.
- **:1157** was split into two sentences; the comma splice garbled it aloud.
- **KT10 left as written.** Its "under Hockey Canada always does" is harsher than the book only when a charge reaches the head, which is the conservative direction, and safety ruled it safe voiced alone.

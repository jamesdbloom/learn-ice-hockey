### rules_primer :400: ban reach and slur (penalty claim)
- **Before:** "The ban reaches every league or age group you are registered in, and a discriminatory slur brings an immediate suspension (IHUK Department of Player Safety Rules and Procedures, Appendix C and Section 8)."
- **After:** "You serve the ban in full in the league where it happened, and miss one game in every other league or age group you are registered in (IHUK Department of Player Safety Rules and Procedures, Section 11 and Appendix C.1). A discriminatory slur is at least a one-game ban too, and brings an immediate provisional suspension, which you sit out while it is investigated (Appendix C.1 and Section 8)."
- **Sources** (all in sources/eih_dops_2026-27_layout.txt):
  - §11 :176-177: "served in full in the league where the offence occurred, and the player must also miss one game in every other league or age group".
  - C.1 :411-416: the slur is among the "Mandatory IIHF one-game bans".
  - §8 :151-153: "an immediate provisional suspension pending investigation".
- **How the text got here.** "Reaches every league" overstated the ban, which is served in full only in the league of the offence. The author's first draft added "provisional" but dropped the one-game ban from the slur sentence, and safety ruled that a Major because it read cheaper. The final text combines three findings:
  - **Rules:** "one game", not "at least one".
  - **Content:** split into two claims, each with its own citation.
  - **Safety:** the slur's one-game ban restored.
- **rules_primer :595 and mental_game :577.** Both now read "an immediate provisional suspension, which you sit out while it is investigated, and at least a one-game ban", matching uk_rules :415.

### mental_game: "Spitting and slurs." and "Threats."
- **Split.** The bullet "Spitting, slurs and threats" (1,195 characters) was split at the threats sentence, so the provisional wording would fit under the 1,200 cap. "Spitting and slurs." now leads with "Never spit at anyone, and never use a slur."
- **"Threats." final text:** "Never threaten anyone with harm, however angry you are — you do not have to touch anyone to be thrown out. Threatening an official is a match penalty under USA Hockey, Hockey Canada and CARHA, and USA Hockey's match also covers threatening an opposing player or team official, with a likely multi-game suspension (601(e)(2)). Under Hockey Canada, threatening anyone else can end your game straight away (11.2); under CARHA, abusive words or gestures at anyone else are a misconduct, and a game misconduct if you keep at it (46(b)). In the NHL, PWHL and IIHF, throwing a stick or shooting the puck at an official counts as threatening one and brings a suspension — at least three games in the NHL (40.4)."
- **Why it changed.** Once split out as its own spoken unit, the official-only text was found to be permissive:
  - USA Hockey 601(e)(2) also reaches "opposing player" (content Major).
  - It was silent for the NHL, PWHL and IIHF (safety Minor).
- **How the draft was corrected.** The first expansion was in turn corrected by the final reads:
  - Hockey Canada 11.2 Note 1 allows a game misconduct without a prior misconduct (rules and content).
  - CARHA 46(f) is the narrow incite-after-warning limb. 46(b)(1) prices "abusive language or gestures to any person" outright (rules).
  - "anyone else", so the official stays on the match penalty (HC 11.5(c)(i), CARHA 71(c)) (safety, twice).
  - The PWHL 40.4 minimum is one game, not three (author, against the sketch).
  - The content reviewer cut a tariff list back to a lead instruction.

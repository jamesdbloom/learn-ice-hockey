## What this method could not have found

Reviewers read diffs plus the layers that restate each claim. A layer built on an old answer, with no lexical tie to the edited text, would pass. So would a book that prices an act under a rule nobody searched. The research agents' verdicts on "never swept" rows rest on act sweeps of the books on disk. Hockey Canada's and CARHA's newer rulebooks are not yet on disk, so nothing here was checked against them.

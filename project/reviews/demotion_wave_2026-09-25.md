## ✅ A retraction — a "wrong citation" row that was not one

**An agent reported a facts line citing USA Hockey 618(c) for a goaltender's forward throw as a wrong citation,
because 618(c) opens with the three-second rule.** 🔴 **618(c) CONTINUES, and a later paragraph is the forward-throw
rule: *"If a goalkeeper catches the puck and throws it forward towards their opponent's goal and it is first played
by a teammate, play shall be stopped…"*** ✅ **The citation is correct; the row is retracted.**
⚠️⚠️ **THE LESSON IS *"READ PAST THE CLOSING MARK"* APPLIED ONE LEVEL DOWN: truncating inside a CLAUSE is the same
failure as truncating inside a SENTENCE.** **Two agents disagreed about this rule and the coordinator verified it
directly rather than relaying either.**

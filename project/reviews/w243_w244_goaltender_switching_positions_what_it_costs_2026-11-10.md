## What changed

- **The goalie's own playing rules stay in place,** because they decide the play: freezing, the trapezoid, out of play, icing, penalty shots, PK, 6-on-5, fights, the 6E–6H rows, the stick handoff and equipment. Their 18 "What it costs." labels became "The price.".
- **Unchanged.** The safety review confirmed that all 56 facts blocks, the 6B–7B rows, every 69.x line and the :1090 quotation fixes are unchanged.
- **New scene sentences.** The running-you, stick and blocker, box-out and goalmouth scenes gained composed scene tiers.

**switching_positions (W244).**
- **New section.** "What it costs" has five subsections: Your head at the dot; Off the dot; Steering, and where checking is barred; Boarding; Fouling a breakaway from behind. The moved paragraphs are verbatim apart from their run-in labels.
- **Unchanged.** The hit-from-behind scene (:298/:300) and the W232/W233 turn-away text.
- **New force-outside paragraph.** The force-outside scene gained a paragraph pricing a body finish where checking is barred and where it is allowed.

**Fixes from review, goaltender:**
- **B1 (safety, BLOCKING).** The new cross-check scene (:1302) put "minimal accidental contact apart" after both Hockey Canada 7.6(b) and 7.7(b), so it read as a Junior and Senior carve-out. The carve-out is Interpretation 2 to 7.6(b) only. 7.7(b) is flat: "will be penalized with a Major penalty and a Game Misconduct penalty, whether or not injury results". The sentence contradicted :1631 and :1637 in the permissive direction. It now reads "(7.7(b) in Junior and Senior; 7.6(b) in minor and female hockey, where minimal accidental contact is still at least a minor)" (7.6(a)). It also gained the mandatory injury tier (Hockey Canada 9.2(b), CARHA 54(c)).
- **The running-you scene (:1275, rules W1 and safety R1).** "The NHL, IIHF and PWHL can stop at a minor" gave a floor with no ceiling, and it is the only thing the scene says about the IIHF. It now reads "start at a minor but run to a major and game misconduct, and a charge meant to injure you is a match in every book but the IIHF" (NHL and PWHL 42.4 with 21.1, USA Hockey 602(a) and 607(e), Hockey Canada 8.5(c), CARHA 48(a); the IIHF writes no match). Common Mistakes :1697 carried the same floor-only sentence and gets the same fix, from the narrow re-check. The head counterweight gained "or it was meant to injure".
- **Goalmouth (:1380, safety R3).** Driving a player head first into the post now names the match under Hockey Canada 7.5(c) and CARHA 53(b) when they cannot protect themselves. It is split into its own sentence so "a match" cannot attach to the wrong book.
- **Box-out (:1379, rules W3).** CARHA 49(a) is scoped "where the contact was intentional".
- **"Harsher of" recast as "plan on" (rules W4).** At :1625, :1627 and CM :1698 the "harsher of" readings are now framed as instructions, not as the book's own pricing.
- **IIHF carve-out reverted (narrow re-check, BLOCKING).** The coordinator had passed on an optional rules wording that added "and the IIHF" to the "while it stays off your head" carve-out (IIHF 48.1 says it supersedes other head-hit rules, and 48.2 is a minor). In three places the same page says "under the IIHF … never plan on a minor", once inside the same Common Mistakes bullet, so the page contradicted itself. The coordinator removed the addition at all three sites. That keeps the conservative planning instruction, which matches the owner. The unresolved IIHF 43-versus-48 precedence is a plan row.

**Fixes from review, switching_positions:**
- **B1 (safety, BLOCKING).** The new Boarding lead "Ride them wide, then play the puck — the ride is yours, the finish into the wall is not." had been copied from defending_the_rush:449 without its scope, "In a checking league". On this page "ride" means body contact along the wall, so it granted the ride to every reader under USA Hockey 604(a), Hockey Canada 7.3, CARHA and the WNIHL. It now opens "In a checking league, …" and adds "Where checking is barred, there is no ride: steer with your route."
- **Force-outside paragraph (:257, safety R1 and R2).** It now gives the floor where checking is barred ("any body check is at least a minor", from 604(c), 7.3(a) and 49(a)), replaces the jargon "a body finish", and adds Hockey Canada 7.2(b)'s mandatory major and game misconduct for boarding that injures. 7.2(b) was also added in the Boarding subsection. HEAD never carried it.
- **F244 (safety R3, rules item 4, narrow re-check).** The rules-verifier's line now voices the Hockey Canada head match and the 608(c) condition ("USA Hockey if reckless and excessive"), at exactly 300 of 300 characters, opening "(Hockey Canada if off the head; IIHF, plan on it)". The 608(c) number now lives in F245 and the scene paragraph.
- **KT8 and the hit-from-behind Common Mistake (safety R4, narrow re-check).** Both are split into sentences so that "plan on it" no longer refers to "a match", and the verbless clause now reads "under Hockey Canada it does while it stays off the head, and on the head it becomes head contact, a match if they cannot protect themselves".
- **Hit-from-behind scene, chunk repacking (commit gate, BLOCKING).** No text in :270 or :272 changed, but moving "USA Hockey alone writes anything resembling a permission…" into What it costs repacked the chunks. The scene's "under Hockey Canada, while it stays off the head" became the last paragraph of chunk 020, and its head-contact counterweight (:272) opened chunk 021. At HEAD both were in chunk 024. The authors' per-chunk rule counts could not see it, because the moved counterweight was the neighbouring paragraph. The scene sentence now carries the counterweight inline, in the wording already reviewed for KT8: "(under Hockey Canada it does while it stays off the head, and on the head it becomes head contact, a match if they cannot protect themselves; under the IIHF, plan on it)". Both now render in chunk 020. Lesson 18 was added to the brief.
- **Pointers (safety R5).** The back-to-back pair was merged. Pointers now read "The full price is under What it costs, near the end of the page."
- **Rules wording.** :180 "costs a minor in most books, and can in the rest". :571 Casebook 623 Situation 3 "a major plus a game misconduct or a match penalty".
- **Optional sentence not added.** Contact between the two centres before the drop is a mandatory minor under USA Hockey 613(e) and Hockey Canada 6.2(c), but CARHA 57(b) could not be located in the extraction, so no three-book claim was written (plan row).

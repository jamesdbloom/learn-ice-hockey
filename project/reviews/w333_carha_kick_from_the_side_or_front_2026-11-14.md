## Source
This wave works one item W332's rules-verifier logged in the slew-foot plan row: CARHA 86(b) reaches only a kicking slew-foot "from behind". The relevant CARHA 2026 text:
- **86(b) and its Case Situation:** a match at the referee's discretion, for a slew-foot "from behind".
- **48(c):** "A player who attempts to kick or deliberately kicks an opposing player shall be assessed a Match penalty". Mandatory, with no direction limit.
- **The glossary's "Kicking":** "deliberately using their skate(s) with a kicking motion to contact an opponent, with no intent to play the puck".

A kick at the skates from the side or front therefore meets 48(c). That is a reading, not a ruling: the glossary's "Tripping" and 86(a)'s "foot … in any manner" also describe a front sweep. So every limb says "plan on a match".

A read-only census found the units that price CARHA through 86(a)/(b) only. It also corrected the plan row: the general kicking units already give CARHA's kick a match. What was missing was the link inside the slew-foot and trip units.

The same census answered the row's other item. Casebook 627 Situation 2 makes intentionally stepping on an opponent a match, and no voiced unit mentions stepping on an opponent. Adding that would be new content, so it went to the plan as a content decision.

Four authors worked the four files: T, U, V and W.

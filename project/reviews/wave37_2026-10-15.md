## Changes, by reason

- **A. Unscoped universals beside the Hockey Canada 7.6 carve-out.**
  - "No bare minor", "ends your night", "every book ends your game" and "never two minutes" are now scoped to a hit that stays on the back, **for Hockey Canada only**.
  - CARHA 50 and USA Hockey have no supersession clause, and CARHA 53's Note forbids substitution. The reviewers caught several scopes that had attached grammatically to every book, and those were moved onto Hockey Canada.
  - The facts blocks in center, winger, body_contact and defender had scoped Hockey Canada "off the head" but carried no head price. Each now has a line: "Once a hit from behind reaches his head or drives him head first into the boards, Hockey Canada prices it as head contact…".
  - A sibling agent caught that the coordinator's first sketch, "Never let a hit from behind ride up to the head", implies that a low hit from behind is fine. It was replaced everywhere.
- **B. One short form.** "…up to a mandatory major and game misconduct, and a match if he could not protect himself" is now used at every summary site. The unconditioned "…and a match" had read as a ceiling.
- **C. The IIHF is not a flat floor.** IIHF 43.3 is "at their discretion, shall assess … who recklessly endangers". "Where the referee judges it reckless" was added, with "no minor (43.2)", in about 20 documents.
- **D. Non-negotiable 6.** About 300 phrases narrating the extraction method were reworded for a reader, or deleted where they described only the extraction. These include "whitespace-flattened", "layout extraction", "on disk", "read by line range", "positive control" and "local primary text". Every negative, scope and date was kept.
- **E. Sweeps by act.**
  - equipment: the broken-stick discard allowance is written by **six** books, not five (PWHL 54.2(iii) Note 2).
  - goaltender: warm-up confinement holds in "every book", not "all four".
  - on_ice_communication KT14: "a hard shove can reach a major … in every book" overstated the NHL and PWHL, where it applies only once it injures or is meant to.
- **Readability.**
  - The DZC 7.6 ladder now appears once, in the owner unit (:177), with short forms elsewhere.
  - Chunk split DZC :177 is fixed and rendered.
  - passing's inherited board-posture limb was demoted out of the summary layers. The positive instruction stays: skates parallel, forearm and hip, head up, never duck. Its Common Mistakes bullet keeps its spoken "Important.".
  - DTR "30.48m(100 ft.)" was verified as printed, by rendering the page.

## What changed

### Hockey Canada's match for hooking, holding and tripping: no 7.1(c), and disclosed as a reading
**The rule position, settled in W385:**
- HC 8.1(c), 8.2(c) and 8.6(c) bar a match.
- The Appendix D chart has no match for these fouls.
- Appendix D Note 1 says a match "shall be assessed for any deliberate attempt to injure".
- 7.1(c) reaches only acts "not otherwise covered within the playing rules".

**Comma-form citations.** The W385 commit gate found the 7.1(c) citation surviving in comma form ("7.1(c), Appendix D Note 1"), undisclosed, at body_contact :410 and :1761 and risk_management :675. Each now cites Note 1 alone, as "this guide's reading".

**Further sites found by sweeping for the claim:**
- **risk_management :704.** It stated flatly that "a trip meant to injure is an attempt to injure under 7.1(c)". It now says the book never settles it. It quotes Note 1, adds the 7.11(c) Interpretation 2 analogy "in extreme cases" (rules R1), and ends "This guide reads that as a match for a trip meant to injure, so plan on one".
- **body_contact :1657 (facts), :1670 and :1773.** These now disclose the reading. :1773 ends "This guide reads that as a match for a hold meant to injure, so plan on one" (safety S1 optional).
- **body_contact :1761 (facts).** Now reads "(8.1(b), CARHA 30(a)), must on injury in both". Safety m3 restored "in both", because CARHA 63(b) is mandatory on injury. The citation changed from 8.1(e) to 8.1(b), the tier clause.
